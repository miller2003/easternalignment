The Triple Clair Method: What Clairvoyance, Clairaudience, and Clairsentience Mean Together

What Each “Clair” Contributes to a Reading

The three psychic channels Seek Chelle names — clairvoyance, clairaudience, and clairsentience — each access different types of information, and understanding what each one does clarifies why their combination is significant.

Clairvoyance (clear seeing) is the most widely referenced of the three. A clairvoyant psychic receives information in the form of visual impressions — images, symbols, scenes, or visions that correspond to aspects of the client’s situation. These images may be literal or symbolic, and interpreting them correctly is the primary skill of the clairvoyant practitioner.

Clairaudience (clear hearing) receives information as auditory input — words, phrases, names, or tones that arise internally and carry meaning about the situation being read. A clairaudient reader may receive a specific name associated with a person in the client’s life, a phrase that encodes something important, or a tone that signals a quality of energy around a situation.

Clairsentience (clear feeling) receives information through felt sense — emotional impressions, physical sensations, and energetic perceptions that correspond to what the people in the reading are experiencing. A clairsentient reader can perceive the emotional state of a third party without that person being present, which is particularly relevant for love and relationship questions where the client’s central concern is what another person is actually feeling.

Why the Combination Matters — Reading Through Three Simultaneous Channels

Each clair, operating alone, provides a partial picture. A purely clairvoyant reading delivers visual information that must then be interpreted. A purely clairaudient reading delivers auditory fragments that require contextualisation. A purely clairsentient reading delivers emotional impressions that may lack specificity.

When all three operate simultaneously — as Seek Chelle’s profile indicates is the case for her — the channels cross-reference and complete each other. A visual impression can be confirmed or clarified by an auditory detail. An emotional perception can be given specific shape by a corresponding image. The information that arrives through one channel can anchor what arrives through another, producing a more complete and internally consistent picture of the situation than any single channel could generate alone.

This is the structural mechanism behind her claim to read what is unspoken. An emotion that the client has not articulated — has not even consciously accessed — will still register clairsentiently. A name or phrase the client was afraid to introduce will still arrive clairaudiently. A dynamic the client cannot see from inside their situation will still appear clairvoyantly. The triple clair combination does not depend on what the client volunteers. It reads what is actually present.

”Tuning Into What’s Unspoken” — How the Triple Clair Accesses the Real Question

The practical consequence of reading through three simultaneous channels is precisely what Seek Chelle names: access to what the client leaves unspoken. The client may ask about the future of a relationship while the real question is whether they are being lied to in the present. The client may frame a question around career while the underlying concern is a loss of identity. The client may ask a surface question as a test — to see if the reader can find the real one without being told.

In each case, a reader working from what the client explicitly provides will address the stated question and miss the real one. A reader with triple clair capacity receives information through channels that are not mediated by the client’s verbal framing — and is therefore not limited to what the client chose to say. This is why Seek Chelle’s review record so consistently mentions that she understood the real situation without being told: that is the triple clair method functioning as described.

The Birthdate Protocol: Why Seek Chelle Asks for Names and Dates of Birth

What Birthdates Actually Do in an Energy-Based Reading

Seek Chelle is unusual among Kasamba’s energy-based readers in that she has a specific and publicly stated preparation protocol. Before the session, she requests the first names and full dates of birth of anyone the client wants to include in the reading. This is not incidental — it reflects a deliberate methodological position about how the energy connection is established and how its accuracy is maintained.

In an energy-based reading, the reader is attempting to connect with the energetic field of one or more specific individuals. Names provide an initial focal point, but they are imprecise — there are many people with any given name. A date of birth is unique to an individual and carries specific numerological and astrological information that functions as a precise energetic signature. Providing it does not tell the reader what to think about a person; it gives her a specific point of connection to that person’s energy that improves the precision of what she perceives.

The birthdate protocol is also a signal about Seek Chelle’s working standards. Readers who have no preparation requirements are either working from sufficiently broad intuition that specificity is unnecessary, or they are working from what the client provides during the session rather than from independent perception. A reader who actively requests precise energetic identifiers before beginning is signalling that her method depends on genuine connection to a specific individual — and that she needs the right information to make that connection accurately.

Who to Include — and What Level of Detail Helps

The most useful approach is to provide the birthdate of anyone whose thoughts, feelings, or intentions are central to the client’s question. For a love reading involving a specific person, that person’s birthdate. For a question involving a workplace relationship, the relevant colleague or supervisor. For a family situation, the relevant family member.

If a birthdate is genuinely unknown — which happens — providing the name alone still gives her something to work with. The reading will proceed; the precision may be somewhat reduced. Do not invent or estimate a birthdate if the real one is unavailable. Inaccurate information in a preparation protocol creates noise rather than signal.

How to Prepare Before the Session Starts

Because Seek Chelle has an explicit preparation protocol, the session itself moves more efficiently when that preparation is done in advance rather than during the opening minutes. Have the relevant names and birthdates written down and ready to provide as soon as the session connects. Alongside the birthdates, have clarity about the primary question — not a script, but a clear sense of what situation most needs insight. Seek Chelle’s triple clair method will access what is unspoken, but giving the reading a conscious starting point helps orient the session before the deeper layers emerge.

Before you connect with an advisor, you must understand the structural limits and advantages of a psychic reading. A legitimate reader is not hacking into your partner’s brain, nor are they overriding free will to cast a “return” spell. They are reading energy, momentum, and psychological blocks.

The Power of “Specific Person” Readings

When you ask about a specific person—an ex, a current partner, or a complicated situationship—a skilled clairsentient or telepathic reader can bypass the things that person says and tune directly into what they actually feel. This is critical when you are dealing with an avoidant partner or someone who has shut down communication. The reading acts as a diagnostic tool, identifying the core fear or external pressure that is driving their behavior.

Timelines vs. Trajectories

Many clients demand exact dates: “When will he text me?” A credible reader will rarely give you a fixed, unchangeable date. Time in the spiritual sense is fluid and entirely dependent on human choices. Instead, the best readers provide trajectories. They map the current momentum of the connection and tell you where it is highly probable to land if current conditions hold. They will also identify what actions you are taking that might be delaying or accelerating that outcome.

The Warning Signs of a Bad Reading

You are the consumer, and consumer protection is your responsibility. Disconnect immediately if a reader:

  1. Promises a 100% guarantee that an ex will return.
  2. Tells you that a “curse” or “dark energy” is keeping you apart, which they can conveniently remove for an additional fee.
  3. Spends your paid minutes asking you leading questions rather than providing insights.

FAQs

Can a Kasamba psychic really tell me if my ex is coming back?

Yes, a skilled clairvoyant can read the current trajectory of the relationship and the internal state of your ex. However, they cannot override free will. They will tell you the highest probability based on current energy, but if your ex fundamentally chooses to walk away, a legitimate psychic will tell you the uncomfortable truth rather than selling you a false reunion fantasy.

Why do readers like Love Stefans charge $20 a minute?

Online psychic platforms operate on a free-market pricing model. Readers set their own rates based on demand. A reader with 134,000 sessions has a massive base of returning clients willing to pay that premium. High rates typically indicate extreme consistency and demand, allowing the reader to take fewer, higher-quality sessions.

Is Tarot or pure Clairvoyance better for love readings?

Neither is inherently better; it depends on your preference. Pure clairvoyance (like Seek Chelle or Love Stefans) is faster and more direct, as the reader receives visions immediately. Tarot (like Cosmic Fusion) provides a structural framework that some clients find easier to follow and understand visually.
