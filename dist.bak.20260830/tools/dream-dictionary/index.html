Why Dreams Use Symbols

Dream analysts across psychological and spiritual traditions agree on one thing: the dreaming mind rarely speaks literally. It translates your unresolved emotions, fears, and desires into images your waking mind can hold. The same symbol can mean different things for different people — which is why a dictionary gives you the common meanings, and a live reader helps you find the meaning for you.

From Symbol to Session

Some of the most powerful psychic readings start with a recurring dream. If a symbol keeps returning — especially an ex, a deceased loved one, or a house you no longer live in — the dream may be carrying a message worth taking to a reader. The mediums and dream interpreters I've tested on Kasamba, Purple Garden, and Keen work with exactly this material, and all three offer introductory trial minutes.
