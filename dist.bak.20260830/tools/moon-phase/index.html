What the Moon Phases Mean

In spiritual and magical practice, the moon's cycle is a calendar of intention. The new moon is for setting intentions and beginning projects; the waxing phases are for building momentum; the full moon is for culmination, release, and clarity; the waning phases are for letting go. Many seekers time their rituals, their decisions, and even their readings to the lunar cycle.

The moon also drives some of the most emotionally charged timing questions in the psychic world — reconciliation windows, "when will I hear from him," and major life decisions all get asked against the lunar backdrop. If your question feels tied to timing, the readers I've tested on Kasamba, Purple Garden, and Keen can layer lunar timing onto your specific situation.

How This Tool Works

The phase is computed using the standard synodic month (29.53 days) anchored to the known new moon of January 6, 2000. The calculation is accurate to within a fraction of a day for current dates. Illumination is the fraction of the moon's disc lit as seen from Earth.

Use the Moon for Your Next Reading

If you book a reading during a full moon, the energy is at its peak for clarity and release; during a new moon, it is at its best for intention-setting. Either way, come with one focused question — my question guide will help you shape it.
