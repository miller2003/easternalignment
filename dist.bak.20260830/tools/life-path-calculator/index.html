Life Path Number Calculator

Your Life Path number is the most significant number in numerology — it describes the path you are here to walk. Enter your birth date and the calculator reduces it to a single digit (or master number).

What Is a Life Path Number?

The Life Path number is the core of a numerology reading. It is calculated by reducing your full birth date to a single digit (or one of the master numbers, 11, 22, and 33). It describes your strengths, your challenges, and the general direction of your life — not a fixed destiny, but the path your energy naturally takes.

The Meanings

  • 1 — The Leader: independence, initiative, originality.
  • 2 — The Diplomat: cooperation, sensitivity, balance.
  • 3 — The Creator: expression, joy, social energy.
  • 4 — The Builder: order, hard work, stability.
  • 5 — The Freedom Seeker: change, adventure, adaptability.
  • 6 — The Nurturer: responsibility, love, community.
  • 7 — The Seeker: analysis, spirituality, depth.
  • 8 — The Achiever: power, ambition, material success.
  • 9 — The Humanitarian: compassion, completion, wisdom.
  • 11 — The Visionary: intuition, inspiration, master teacher.
  • 22 — The Master Builder: manifesting large-scale dreams.
  • 33 — The Master Teacher: unconditional service and healing.

Beyond the Number

Your Life Path number is one layer of a chart that also includes your Expression, Soul Urge, and Personality numbers — and, in the psychic world, the energetic read of what those layers mean for your relationships and decisions. A live reading can layer numerology, tarot, and intuition onto your specific chart.
