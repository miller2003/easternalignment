How Compatibility Scoring Works

This calculator uses classical astrological rules: signs of the same element share a natural resonance, harmonious elements (Fire-Air, Earth-Water) flow together, challenging elements (Fire-Water, Earth-Air) create friction that can become growth, and opposite signs form the most magnetic — and most demanding — axis in the zodiac. The full methodology is in my zodiac compatibility hub.

What the Score Means

A high score means the two signs have natural chemistry and shared rhythm. A challenging score is not a verdict — some of the most transformative relationships in astrology are built on friction, because each partner forces the other to grow. The score is the starting point, not the conclusion.

Sun Signs Are Just the Beginning

Sun sign compatibility tells you the potential between two signs. Your full birth charts — moon, rising, Venus, Mars — tell you the specifics of the connection. A live reading can compare your actual charts, identify the hidden friction points, and read the timing of the relationship.
