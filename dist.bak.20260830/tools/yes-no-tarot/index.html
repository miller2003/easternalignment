How a Yes/No Tarot Reading Works

A one-card yes/no draw uses the card's core orientation to answer a binary question. In the simplified system used here, upright cards pull toward "yes" and reversed cards pull toward "no" — but the meaning is never mechanical. The card also carries a message about why, which is usually the more useful part of the answer.

The tool draws from a curated 22-card Major Arcana deck. The Major Arcana covers the big themes of life — love, career, change, identity — which makes it ideal for questions that actually matter.

How to Ask a Good Yes/No Question

  • Keep it binary: "Should I take this job?" beats "What should I do about work?"
  • Make it about you: "Is this relationship right for me?" rather than "Is he coming back?"
  • Ask once. Repeating the same question invites the same answer with different packaging.

When a Card Reading Is Not Enough

A single card gives you a direction. A live psychic reading gives you context — timing, obstacles, the other person's energy, and the specific path forward.
