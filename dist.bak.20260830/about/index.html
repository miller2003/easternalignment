Hi, I'm Sarah

Spiritual wellness researcher and the person behind every review on Eastern Alignment.

How It All Started

Three years ago, I was going through the darkest period of my life. A toxic relationship had ended, I was questioning every decision I'd ever made, and I felt completely unmoored. Traditional therapy was helping with the clinical side, but I needed something else — something that could help me feel connected to a bigger picture.

On a sleepless Tuesday night at 2 AM, I found myself typing "online psychic reading" into Google. Half skeptical, half desperate. I signed up for the first platform I found and connected with a psychic who would unknowingly change the trajectory of my life.

She didn't tell me what I wanted to hear. She told me what I needed to hear. And the startling accuracy of her observations — things she couldn't possibly have known — made me realize there was something genuinely valuable happening here, beyond what my rational mind could easily explain.

Why I Started This Site

After that first experience, I went on a deep dive. I tried every major psychic reading platform I could find. Some were incredible. Some were mediocre. A few were outright disappointing. And I realized that there was a massive gap in the market: honest, first-hand reviews from someone who had actually used these services as a real customer.

Most "review" sites I found were either:

  • Thinly veiled advertisements written by people who clearly never had a reading
  • Overly skeptical takedowns dismissing the entire field
  • Generic listicles recycling the same information from each other

I wanted to create something different. A resource that treats seekers with respect, shares genuine experiences (including the bad ones), and helps people navigate this space without wasting their money or, worse, being taken advantage of.

My Approach

Every review on Eastern Alignment follows the same process:

  1. I sign up personally — creating a real account just like you would
  2. I pay out of my own pocket — no free readings from platforms
  3. I do multiple readings — across different psychics and specialties
  4. I take detailed notes — on accuracy, communication, value, and overall experience
  5. I revisit and update — platforms change, and so do my reviews

A Note on Honesty

Yes, this site contains affiliate links. When you sign up for a platform through my links, I may earn a commission. This is how I fund the site and continue doing this work.

But here's what I want you to know: the affiliate relationship never influences my reviews. I've given mediocre ratings to platforms that pay higher commissions, and I've enthusiastically recommended platforms with lower affiliate payouts. My recommendations are based on one thing only: which platforms provided the best experience for real people seeking genuine guidance.

If a platform isn't good, I'll tell you. If I had a bad reading, you'll hear about it. My credibility is the only asset I have, and I'm not willing to trade it for a bigger commission check.

What I Believe

I believe that psychic readings, at their best, are a tool for self-reflection and empowerment — not a crutch or a crystal ball. The best psychics I've encountered help you see your own situation more clearly, not create dependence.

I believe everyone deserves access to honest information before spending their money. And I believe this industry, like any other, has both genuine talent and opportunists — my job is to help you tell the difference.
