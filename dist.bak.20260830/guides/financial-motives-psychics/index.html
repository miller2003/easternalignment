In the modern dating world, a terrifying new archetype has emerged: The Hobo-Sexual.

This is a partner who enters a relationship and says “I love you,” not because they are romantically obsessed with you, but because they are romantically obsessed with your spare bedroom, your stable income, and your willingness to pay for dinner. The “Dimensional Strike”: How Psychics Catch Parasites

Before you call a psychic to check your partner’s motives, you must understand the esoteric architecture of financial exploitation.

1. The “Root Chakra” Siphon

The Root Chakra governs physical survival: shelter, food, and money. When an amateur psychic reads your partner, they might say, “He feels very safe and comfortable with you.” A master psychic will look deeper and say, “He feels safe because his Root Chakra has attached itself to your wallet like a parasite.” A master understands the difference between a man who feels safe because of emotional trust, and a man who feels safe because you are funding his survival.

2. The 8th House of “Other People’s Money”

In Astrology, the 2nd House rules your own money, but the 8th House rules “Other People’s Money” (inheritances, debts, and your spouse’s income). If you pull an astrological Synastry chart and see that your partner’s chart heavily activates your 8th House, it means they are karmically engineered to drain your resources. An elite astrologer can warn you of this before you ever let them move in.

3. The Tarot’s “Seven of Swords”

The Tarot is notoriously blunt about money. If your partner is hiding secret credit card debt, gambling losses, or secretly saving money in a private account so they can leave you once they get a promotion, the cards will reveal it. The appearance of the Seven of Swords (Deception/Theft) alongside pentacles (money) is the ultimate esoteric red flag.

How to Ask a Psychic About Financial Exploitation

If you want the truth, you must remove the romance from the reading.

1. Treat it Like a Business Audit Do not say, “I love him so much, but he’s going through a hard time financially. Is he going to be okay?” (This makes the psychic pity him). Say: “I am the sole financial provider right now. I need you to look at his Root Chakra and his hidden intentions. Is he actively trying to rebuild his own independence, or is he comfortably using my resources as a permanent safety net?”

2. Ask for the Exit Timeline A true financial parasite often has an exit plan. Ask the psychic: “If I were to lose my job tomorrow and the money stopped, would his energy remain anchored to me, or would he immediately sever the connection?” The answer to that question will tell you everything you need to know.
