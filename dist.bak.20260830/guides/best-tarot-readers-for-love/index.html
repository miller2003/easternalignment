When navigating a complex romantic situation—an unexplained breakup, a confusing situationship, or a stagnant marriage—many clients turn to online psychics. But there is a massive structural difference between calling a pure clairvoyant and consulting a Tarot Reader.

A pure clairvoyant asks you to trust the invisible visions in their head. A Tarot reader provides a visible, archetypal map. Tarot is not magic; it is a profound psychological and energetic framework that has been refined over centuries to reflect human behavior. When used by a master, the 78 cards of a Tarot deck will bypass your partner’s lies, your own denial, and the surface-level noise to show you exactly what is operating beneath the surface.

However, the psychic industry is flooded with amateurs who bought a deck of cards last week and are reading generic meanings out of a guidebook at $5 a minute.

To find the actual Tarot masters, we audited the databases of the two largest online psychic networks: Keen and Kasamba. How to Choose the Right Tarot Style for Your Situation

Before you connect with a reader, you must understand that there are two entirely different ways to read Tarot. Picking the wrong style for your personality will result in a frustrating reading.

1. “Card-by-Card” vs. “Intuitive Pull”

  • Card-by-Card Structure: Readers who lay out a formal spread (like the Celtic Cross) and interpret the exact positional meaning of each card. This is excellent for clients who are highly logical and want to understand why the reader is making a prediction. (Best example: Master Sher).
  • Intuitive Pull: Readers who use the cards simply as a jumping-off point. They draw three cards, catch an energetic signature, and then ditch the formal structure to follow the psychic thread. This is best for clients dealing with highly emotional, complex emotional entanglements. (Best example: Psychic Yazmin).

2. The Deck Matters (Rider-Waite vs. Inclusive Decks)

The imagery on the cards fundamentally limits or expands the reading. If you are an LGBTQ+ client asking about a complex queer dynamic, a reader using a traditional, heteronormative deck will have to constantly translate the imagery, introducing friction into the reading. Readers who actively utilize inclusive tools (like Tarot With Meg’s use of the Queer Tarot) provide infinitely more accurate readings for non-traditional relationships.

How to Get the Best Tarot Reading for Your Money

If you are paying by the minute, you cannot afford to waste time. Follow these two rules to maximize your ROI:

1. Don’t Pay Them to Shuffle (Use Chat) If you are using Keen (which is phone-dominant), be aware that some traditional Tarot readers take 30-60 seconds to shuffle and lay out the cards. At $5/minute, you are paying $5 to listen to silence. To avoid this, use Kasamba→’s Chat feature, or tell your Keen reader the question the moment they pick up the phone so they can shuffle while you talk.

2. Ask “Open” Tarot Questions, Not “Binary” Ones Tarot is a narrative tool, not a coin flip.

  • Wrong Question: “Will he text me this weekend?” (This forces a complex 78-card deck into a simple yes/no, which is a waste of the tool).
  • Right Question: “What is the current energy blocking his communication, and what is the likely outcome if I reach out first?” (This allows the Tarot cards to map the psychology, the blockage, and the trajectory).
