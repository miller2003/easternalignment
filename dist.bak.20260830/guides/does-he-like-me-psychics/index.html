The “Dimensional Strike”: How Psychics Expose the Talking Stage

Before you spend money to find out if your crush likes you, you must understand the pitfalls of reading new energy.

1. Lust vs. Longevity (The Chakra Trap)

A low-tier psychic will look at a guy who just wants to sleep with you and excitedly tell you, “Yes! He is highly attracted to you!” This is dangerous. A master psychic differentiates between energy centers. They will tell you: “His Root Chakra (physical lust) is highly activated towards you, but his Heart Chakra (emotional commitment) is completely closed.” You need a reader who knows the difference between a fling and a future.

2. The “Mirroring” Danger

When you are desperately crushing on someone, your energy field is massive and loud. If you call an amateur psychic, they will accidentally read your intense desire to be loved, and feed it back to you, mistakenly claiming it is his feelings. The elite readers on this list are trained to separate your anxious energy from his actual intent.

3. Diagnosing “Hot and Cold” Behavior

Why do guys pull away right after a great date? Often, it is an Avoidant Attachment trigger. An empathic psychic will bypass his aloof behavior and read the underlying panic. They can tell you if he is pulling away because he is losing interest, or if he is pulling away because he is terrified of how much he actually likes you.

How to Ask a Psychic About a New Crush

If you want an accurate reading on a new relationship, you must manage your own energy during the session.

1. Do Not Gush About Him If you call the psychic and spend three minutes talking about how perfect he is, how handsome he is, and how much you want to marry him, you are contaminating the reading. The psychic will pick up on your massive desire and feed it back to you.

2. Ask Neutral, Diagnostic Questions Instead of asking, “Are we going to fall in love?” (which is desperate), ask: “I recently met [Name]. What is his current intent toward me, and is he emotionally available for a serious relationship?” This forces the psychic to look at his reality, rather than your fantasy.
