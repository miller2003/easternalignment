The Short Version: What I Recommend After 3+ Years of Testing

If you only read one section of this page, read this one. After years of paid testing across the major platforms — dozens of sessions, hundreds of documented hours, and every platform reviewed below evaluated with my own money — the honest conclusion is that there is no single "best" psychic platform. Kasamba is my #1 overall recommendation for most people in 2026. Quality is visible on Keen if you are willing to look, and its 5 minutes for $1 trial lets you test a top-tier phone reader for pocket change.

The common thread in every platform I recommend: they let you verify. The platforms I tell people to avoid are the ones that hide their rosters, scrub negative reviews, or monetize fear. I have documented the scam patterns in my fake psychic guide — and the single most useful habit you can build is treating every reading as a hypothesis to be checked, not a verdict to be obeyed.

Common Questions

Are online psychic readings legitimate?

Many online platforms employ screened, experienced advisors who provide genuine intuitive guidance. The key is choosing reputable platforms that vet their psychics and offer satisfaction guarantees. My reviews are based on personal testing — real accounts, real money, documented sessions — to help you distinguish quality from pretenders.

How much does an online psychic reading cost?

Prices typically range from $0.99 to $30+ per minute, depending on the platform, the modality, and the reader’s experience. Entry-level chat runs $1–$5/min, professional readers with track records run $5–$15/min, and elite specialists run $15–$30+/min. Most major platforms offer free or heavily discounted introductory minutes so you can test the connection before committing. See the full <a href="/guides/how-much-does-a-psychic-reading-cost/">pricing guide</a>.

How do you review psychic platforms?

I sign up for and test each platform personally over weeks or months. I evaluate roster quality, pricing transparency, user experience, customer support, satisfaction guarantees, and the accuracy of the readings I actually receive. Where available, I audit public outcome ledgers and session transcripts — for example, my 12-month, $840 audit of Purple Garden tracked 150 Journeys threads.

Which psychic reading platform is best for beginners?

I generally recommend <a href="/go/kasamba" rel="nofollow sponsored" target="_blank">Kasamba</a> for beginners — it is my #1 overall platform in 2026. New clients get <strong>3 free minutes with every new advisor</strong> (plus 50% off the first paid session), so you can test multiple relationship specialists risk-free before spending a dollar, and every chat session is saved as a permanent transcript you can audit later.

Which platform is best for phone readings?

Keen. Its telephone infrastructure is the best in the industry: immediate connections, a flawless callback system for busy readers, and an un-deletable review ledger that makes every advisor’s track record public. If you trust your ears, Keen is the platform.

Which platform is best for chat readings with permanent records?

Kasamba. Every chat session is saved automatically, turning every prediction into a written claim you can audit weeks later. Its 3 free minutes with every new advisor also make it the safest platform for testing many readers risk-free.

Which platform is best for video readings?

Purple Garden. Every advisor is required to post a 60-second video introduction, and its public Journeys ledger lets you audit how clients’ predictions actually landed. It is the only major platform where you can see and inspect an advisor before spending a dollar.

What is the difference between a psychic and a medium?

A psychic perceives information about your circumstances, relationships, or likely path through extrasensory perception. A medium specifically works with people who have died. All mediums are psychics, but not all psychics are mediums — mediumship is a specialized discipline with a much harder standard of evidence. See the full <a href="/guides/psychic-vs-medium-vs-tarot-reader/">breakdown</a>.

How do I avoid psychic scams?

The industry’s scam patterns are consistent: manufactured fear (curses, dark energy, blocks), escalating paid “removals,” guaranteed outcomes, and the waiting loop that keeps clients paying month after month. A legitimate reading leaves you clearer, not more afraid. I’ve documented every pattern in the <a href="/guides/how-to-spot-fake-psychic/">fake psychic detection guide</a>.

How do I prepare for my first reading?

Write down one focused question before the session, give the reader minimal backstory, and let them demonstrate unprompted specificity before you confirm anything. The question quality determines the reading quality. My <a href="/guides/questions-to-ask-a-psychic/">question guide</a> and <a href="/guides/first-psychic-reading-guide/">first-reading guide</a> walk through the whole protocol.
