Understanding the Importance of Your Horoscope

When reading your daily horoscope, it's unlikely that you're thinking about the fact that the Babylonians developed their own version of this practice around 2,400 years ago — but that's just how long the human race has been looking to the stars to find answers regarding present and future circumstances.

Using a chart of the heavens, an astrologer casts a horoscope based on a specific astrological sign. This chart shows the corresponding positions of the sun, moon, and planets, as well as the ruling signs of the zodiac that coincide with one's birth time. From here, astrologers can evaluate a person's character and make predictions from the future.

More often than not, the heavens are illustrated by a circle divided into 12 intersections known as houses. Each house represents a different aspect of life such as career, finances, marriage, career, relationships, etc. It's believed that the planet that falls within each house has a major influence on it. This is why there are different types of horoscopes that cater to these specific areas. There's even a birthday horoscope that sheds light on the year ahead.

So you see, your free daily horoscope is just a small piece of the astrological pie! Everything in the universe is interrelated and there are many factors that can alter your future.
