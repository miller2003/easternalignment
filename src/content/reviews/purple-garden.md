## 2. The Game-Changer: 60-Second Video Profile Bios

In all my years of getting psychic readings, the single biggest financial drain is what I call the **"Cold Energetic Mismatch."** You pay $5 or $6 a minute for an advisor whose written bio sounds amazing, only to connect on the phone and realize within 60 seconds that their communication style is rushed, dismissive, or completely incompatible with your temperament. On older platforms, you've already spent $15 just discovering that you don't mesh.

Purple Garden completely solves this through **Pre-Recorded Video Introductions**.

Every serious advisor on Purple Garden uploads a 30- to 60-second video introduction to their profile. Before you deposit a cent, you can tap play and observe them in their actual working environment.

### What I Look For When Screening Video Bios:
During my 12-month test, I developed a strict 3-point checklist when watching an advisor's video bio:
1. **Eye Contact & Vocal Grounding:** Does the advisor speak with calm, unhurried authenticity, or are they performing a theatrical script? Genuine empaths have a steady, reassuring cadence.
2. **The Workspace Hygiene:** Are they sitting at a quiet spiritual desk with worn tarot decks, candles, or Lenormand cards? Or are they recording from a noisy kitchen or a blurry digital background?
3. **Absence of Fear-Based Marketing:** If an advisor mentions "dark blockages," "curses," or guarantees "100% lover return," I swipe away immediately.

By spending ten minutes watching video bios, I was able to filter out mediocre readers for free before booking my first paid session.

## 3. The "Journeys" Feed: The Ultimate Anti-Scam Ledger

If video bios are Purple Garden's front door, **Journeys** is its engine room. This feature alone separates Purple Garden from almost every other platform on the internet.

On most review sites, clients leave a 5-star rating right after the reading ends—usually because the psychic was polite or told them what they wanted to hear. But what happens three weeks later when the predicted reconciliation never happens? On legacy sites, that initial 5-star review remains locked in place.

### How Journeys Changes the Equation
Journeys is an in-app social timeline where seekers share their readings and—crucially—**return weeks or months later to post verification updates.**

When I tested Purple Garden, I bookmarked 40 Journeys threads across top advisors. I watched real clients post entries like:

Because advisors cannot edit or delete client Journeys updates, you can quickly see which readers consistently nail real-world timelines versus those who issue generic "everything will work out" platitudes.
