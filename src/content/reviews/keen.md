## What Is Keen?

Keen has been connecting seekers with psychic advisors since 1999, making it one of the veterans in the online psychic space. While it operates in the same market as Kasamba and Psychic Source, Keen has carved out its own niche with competitive pricing and what I consider the best mobile app experience of any psychic platform.

## How I Tested Keen

Over the course of two years, I've had **15+ readings** on Keen, focusing primarily on:

- **Phone readings** (their strongest format)
- **Love and relationship guidance**
- **Life path and career advice**
- **Tarot readings**

I approached each session with the same questions I used on other platforms, allowing me to compare the quality of insights directly.

## First Impressions

What struck me first about Keen was how straightforward the platform feels. There's no pretense or over-the-top mystical branding. It's clean, professional, and gets you connected with a psychic quickly.

The advisor profiles are informative, showing real user reviews, areas of expertise, and pricing upfront. I appreciate that there's no hidden cost structure — what you see is what you pay.

## The Phone Reading Experience

Where Keen truly shines is in phone readings. The call quality is consistently clear, there's no lag, and the connection feels private and personal. Several of the phone psychics I connected with were warm, intuitive, and genuinely focused on helping me rather than keeping me on the line longer.

One psychic in particular gave me career advice that turned out to be eerily accurate — she suggested I'd receive an unexpected opportunity within three weeks, and I did.

## The Mobile App

Keen's mobile app deserves special mention. It's one of the best in the industry:

- Easy to browse and filter psychics
- Quick connection for phone or chat readings
- Push notifications when your favorite psychic comes online
- Clean, intuitive design

If you prefer doing your readings from your phone (and let's be honest, most of us do), Keen's app makes the experience seamless.

## Pricing

Keen's pricing is competitive:

- **Introductory psychics**: $1.99 – $4.99/min
- **Experienced advisors**: $5.00 – $9.99/min
- **Premium advisors**: $10.00 – $20.00/min

The introductory offer gives you your first 3 minutes free with any new psychic, which is a great way to test the chemistry before committing to a longer session.

## Where Keen Falls Short

Being honest, there are a few areas where Keen doesn't quite match the competition:

1. **Smaller roster**: You won't find the sheer volume of psychics that Kasamba offers
2. **Chat experience**: The chat interface works but feels less polished than the competition
3. **Availability gaps**: If you're looking for a reading at 3 AM, your options are more limited

## Who Is Keen Best For?

Keen works best for:

✓ **People who prefer phone readings** — this is their strongest format  
✓ **Budget-conscious seekers** looking for affordable options  
✓ **Mobile users** who want a great app experience  
✓ **Anyone who values a straightforward, no-nonsense platform**

## The Bottom Line

Keen is a solid, reliable psychic reading platform that particularly excels in phone readings and mobile accessibility. While it doesn't have the massive roster of Kasamba, it makes up for it with competitive pricing, an excellent app, and a strong satisfaction guarantee.

If phone readings are your preferred format, Keen should be near the top of your list.
