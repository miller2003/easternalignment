## 1. Architectural Clash: Auditory Telephony vs. Visual Resonance

To understand why a reader might be terrifyingly accurate on Keen but fail on Purple Garden (or vice versa), you must look at how the medium shapes intuitive perception.

### Keen: The Telephony Giant (Pure Voice)
Founded in 1999, **Keen** was engineered around traditional telephone routing. While they offer web chat, over 70% of high-value sessions on Keen occur via voice call.
- **The Cognitive Advantage:** Voice carries profound energetic frequency. A genuine clairaudient or empath on Keen relies on real-time vocal modulation—the micro-tremor in your voice, the pauses, the breathing patterns—to lock onto your energetic baseline immediately.
- **Who Thrives Here:** Direct temporal timeline readers and mediums. Advisors on Keen tend to get straight to the point without visual distractions.

### Purple Garden: The Mobile-First Video Innovator
Backed by the creators of Purple Ocean and BitWine, **Purple Garden** was designed from day one around smartphones and **Live Video Readings**.
- **The Cognitive Advantage:** Visual transparency eliminates the greatest fear seekers have: paying an anonymous stranger who might be reading from a script. On Purple Garden, you watch the advisor shuffle physical tarot cards, observe their spiritual environment, and look into their eyes in real time.
- **Who Thrives Here:** Visual tarot practitioners, aura readers, and seekers who need facial validation to feel grounded and safe during a vulnerable consultation.

On Purple Garden, every serious advisor uploads a pre-recorded video introduction. Within forty-five seconds of watching an advisor speak on camera, your intuition will tell you everything you need to know:
- Do they sound calm, grounded, and spiritually disciplined?
- Or do they sound rushed, theatrical, or commercially scripted?

This single feature prevents more wasted money than any star rating ever could.
