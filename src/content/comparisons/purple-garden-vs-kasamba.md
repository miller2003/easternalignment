## Love & Relationship Dynamics: Video Vetting vs. 3 Free Minutes

When dealing with high-stakes relationship ambiguity—whether an ex-recovery timeline, a situationship crossroad, or a third-party interference—both **Purple Garden** and **Kasamba** are dominant players. However, their risk-mitigation strategies operate on entirely different axes.

---

## Risk Mitigation: How to Vet Without Burning Capital

### Kasamba: 3 Free Minutes Per New Reader
Kasamba remains the industry leader for risk-free vetting because it gives you **3 free chat minutes with *every single new advisor* you consult.** If an advisor spends the first 90 seconds asking broad probing questions instead of delivering unprompted insights, you can terminate the session at zero financial cost.

### Purple Garden: Pre-Session Video Introductions & Journeys
Purple Garden approaches vetting visually. You don't need a 3-minute free trial to sense an advisor's energy because you can watch their **60-second video intro profile** and inspect their **Journeys outcome logs** before initiating contact.

## Accuracy in Relationship Timelines

### Kasamba's Specialist Roster
Kasamba has spent twenty-five years filtering for deep emotional empaths and relationship tarot practitioners. Their top-tier advisors excel at dissecting complex attachment styles and emotional undercurrents.

### Purple Garden's Crowd-Sourced Verification
Purple Garden's **Journeys** feature creates unique accountability. Seekers publicly post whether a reader's predicted reconciliation timeline actually came to pass, allowing you to quickly filter out generalists who give overly optimistic forecasts.

## Final Recommendation

- **Choose [Purple Garden](/reviews/purple-garden/) if** you want face-to-face Live Video readings, lower average per-minute rates, and crowd-sourced timeline auditing via Journeys.
- **Choose [Kasamba](/reviews/kasamba/) if** you prefer text-based chat, want permanent written session transcripts, and plan to exploit the **3 free minutes per new reader** structure to test multiple relationship specialists.
