## How to Handle an Age Gap Reading

When calling a psychic about a May-December romance, set the boundaries immediately.

**1. Own the Gap Upfront**
Do not try to hide the age gap and hope the psychic "guesses" it to prove they are real. State it clearly: *"I am 28, my partner is 52. We are deeply committed. I need you to look at the energetic power dynamic between us regarding our future."* This establishes that you are confident, preventing amateur psychics from trying to shame you.

**2. Focus on "Power," Not "Age"**
The only thing that matters in an age-gap relationship is the power dynamic. Ask the psychic: *"Is there an equal flow of respect and emotional exchange in this connection, or is one partner energetically dominating the other?"* This strips away the biological number and focuses purely on the health of the relationship.
