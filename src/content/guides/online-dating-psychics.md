If you are going to pay a psychic to vet a Tinder match, you must understand the specific esoteric tools they use to catch liars.

## How to Ask a Psychic About an Online Match

If you want to catch a scammer, you must provide the psychic with a clean slate.

**1. Do Not Feed the Psychic His Lies**
Do not say, *"I met this guy who is a doctor in London, but he's stuck in customs and needs $500."* If you give the psychic his fake backstory, it can contaminate the reading. 
Instead, say: *"I met someone online named [Name]. I need a complete energetic background check on his true physical identity and his hidden motives towards me."*

**2. Listen to the Warning**
If a master psychic tells you the person is a phantom, **do not warn the scammer.** Do not confront them. Simply block, delete, and protect your peace.
