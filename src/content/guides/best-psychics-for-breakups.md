## 3 Rules for a Successful Ex-Recovery Reading

If you want to get actual value out of your session, you must stop treating the psychic like a therapist and start treating them like an energetic diagnostician.

### 1. Do Not Give Them the Backstory
When you use your introductory minutes, provide only your ex's first name and date of birth. Do not explain the fight. Do not explain who broke up with who. A legitimate psychic will begin dropping specific details about the dynamic that you did not tell them. If they immediately ask you a string of questions ("Why did you break up?"), they are cold reading. Disconnect.

### 2. Ask the Right Questions
Stop asking binary "yes/no" questions like *"Will he come back?"* Free will changes yes/no outcomes daily. Instead, ask diagnostic questions:
- *"What is currently blocking his communication?"*
- *"Are we repeating a karmic cycle, or is this a new phase?"*
- *"What is my most aligned next step?"*

### 3. Record the Session or Use Chat
In a state of emotional distress, you will suffer from confirmation bias—remembering only what you wanted to hear and forgetting the nuance. Use Kasamba’s Chat feature to automatically generate a transcript, or take meticulous notes during a Keen phone call. Predictions that seem confusing in the moment often become strikingly accurate weeks later.
