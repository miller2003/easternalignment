## How We Tested: The 60+ Reader Audit

This ranking is the output of our full Kasamba review program — 60+ in-depth reader reviews published on this site, each built from the same protocol: profile and session-count verification, review-feed language analysis across hundreds of client comments, negative-review forensics (we read the 1-stars, not just the count), and firsthand session testing on selected readers. From that pool we selected the 12 strongest for love questions specifically, then ranked them within five love-question types.

Three evidence rules governed every pick:

**Prediction receipts beat session feelings.** A reading that felt wonderful proves nothing. We weighted readers whose reviews contain specific, dateable, later-confirmed predictions — the "she said he'd reach out within two weeks, he texted on day nine" class of evidence — above everyone else.

**Repeat clients beat first impressions.** A reader whose feed runs 40%+ repeat clients is surviving on rebookings from people who had time to check the first reading. That behavior cannot be manufactured.

**The negative reviews tell you the type of failure.** "She told me he wasn't coming back and I hated it" is often delayed accuracy. "She kept me on the meter saying nothing" is disqualifying. We read them all.
