## The "Dimensional Strike": How Psychics Calculate Timelines

Before you book a session to ask when you will meet your future spouse, you must understand how a legitimate timeline is constructed.

### 1. The "Readiness Window" vs. Fixed Destiny
A fake psychic will look at a crystal ball and say, *"You will marry a tall man on June 12, 2028."* That is a scam. 

A real esoteric practitioner understands that a marriage timeline is an **Energetic Window**. They look at your chart and say, *"The karmic blockages holding you back will clear by late 2027. Between 2027 and 2028, you will enter a major 7th House transit. That is your readiness window."* The timeline is real, but it requires your free will to walk through the door. If your window opens and you refuse to leave your house, you will miss the transit.

### 2. Why Astrology Beats Clairvoyance for Exact Years
Clairvoyants receive visions, but visions rarely come with a calendar attached. Astrologers, however, use math. By calculating the movement of Jupiter or Saturn across your natal chart, they can pinpoint the exact month a specific energy will enter your life. If you want to know *when*, always book a reader who incorporates Astrology or Numerology into their practice.

### 3. The Power of Hellenistic "Annual Profections"
Ancient Hellenistic Astrology uses a technique called "Annual Profections." This system assigns a specific theme to every year of your life based on your age. If you are entering a "7th House Profection Year," the entire energetic theme of your life will revolve around marriage and serious partnership. Finding a reader who understands this ancient math is the holy grail of timeline prediction.

## How to Prepare for a Timeline Reading

If you want an accurate date, you must provide accurate data.

**1. Have Your Birth Data Ready:**
Do not book an Astrologer or Numerologist if you do not know your birth info. You need your exact **Date of Birth**, **City of Birth**, and ideally, your **Exact Time of Birth** (from your birth certificate). The time of birth dictates your Ascendant and the exact positioning of your 7th House of Marriage. If the time is wrong, the timeline will be wrong.

**2. Stop Asking "Will I" and Start Asking "When"**
Almost everyone has a marriage window in their chart. The question is never *"Will I?"* The question is *"When does the energetic window open, and what inner healing must I complete before then to ensure I don't attract another karmic lesson instead of a spouse?"*
