## FAQs

### Who is the best love psychic online in 2026?

Love Stefans on Kasamba, by the evidence class that matters most: 138,003 readings with the strongest delayed-verification pattern we have audited on any platform — clients returning weeks after difficult sessions to confirm he was right. The platform-specific alternatives: Advisor Suzan (Keen) for phone-first clients, Annah (Purple Garden) for publicly verified dated predictions.

### Which platform is best for love psychics — Kasamba, Keen, or Purple Garden?

Different machines for different shoppers. Kasamba wins on audition economics: 3 free minutes with every new advisor lets you test several readers at zero cost. Keen wins on bench depth: the oldest platform with the largest completed-session counts in the industry, phone-first. Purple Garden wins on verifiability: the Journeys public prediction ledger lets you check a reader's confirmed outcomes before spending. Choose the machine first, then the reader — [our full three-way comparison](/comparisons/keen-vs-kasamba-vs-purple-garden/) has the complete breakdown.

### How much should a love reading cost?

With intro offers: a first test costs $1 (Keen, 5 minutes), nothing (Kasamba, 3 free minutes per advisor), or comes out of $30 credit (Purple Garden, 4–12 minutes depending on rate). Thereafter, a focused 10-minute session runs $20 at budget tiers (Sparks Of Insight, $1.99/min) to $150 at premium tiers (Isabelle, Wisdom and Love). Match the budget to the stakes: a check-in does not need the $150 reader, and the decision-of-your-year does not belong with the $10 one.

### Can an online love psychic really tell me if my ex is coming back?

They can read the current trajectory — what your ex is feeling, whether the connection is live, the conditions around contact resuming. What no legitimate reader sells is certainty: treat "100% guaranteed reunion" as a scam signal on any platform. The readers ranked here are ranked partly on their documented record of accurately saying *no* — which is the reading that saves you the most money.

### Chat, phone, or video for a love reading?

Chat for verification — it is cheapest and leaves a transcript you can check in three weeks (Kasamba is chat-first). Phone for emotional nuance and complex situations — Keen's culture and deepest bench. Video for tool-based readings where watching the process matters — Purple Garden's signature format. When in doubt, start on chat: the transcript is the only format that lets you audit the prediction later.
