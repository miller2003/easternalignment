## The Free Research Protocol: Ten Minutes Before You Spend One

Do this before choosing anyone, on this list or off it. It costs nothing and changes the expected value of your first session more than any other action.

1. **Open the reader's Journeys.** You are not reading for positivity — you are scanning for specific, dateable predictions ("contact within 2 weeks," "the offer arrives in March") with client follow-ups confirming or contradicting. Twenty specific predictions at a 70% confirmation rate beats five hundred "amazing!!!" entries. [The full Journeys reading method is here](/guides/purple-garden-journeys-guide/).
2. **Watch the video bio — for calibration, not charisma.** The bio that describes a method ("I read the current state of a situation and its trajectory") is a green flag. The bio that promises outcomes ("I will tell you exactly what happens") is a red one. Bounded confidence correlates with accuracy; guarantees correlate with meter-running.
3. **Check the recent review names.** Do the same names recur across weeks? Repeat clients are the only audience that cannot be fooled twice. A feed full of one-time reviewers is a feed full of first impressions.
4. **Only then decide how to spend the credit.** The decision tree below maps your situation to the right allocation.

## The Credit Decision Tree: One Credit, Three Strategies

You get to spend the $30 once. Three legitimate ways to do it:

**Strategy A — One deep read (recommended for most first-timers).** Spend the entire credit on one reader with one well-framed question. Best matches: Norell (7.5 min), Namrata (12 min), Tarot Instincts (15 min). This is the highest-information play: a complete session with follow-ups, fully covered.

**Strategy B — Two medium reads.** Split across two readers in different styles — say, Norell (empathic) and Truthful Love (blunt) — ~5 minutes each. Choose this if you genuinely cannot decide between two styles; the comparison itself is educational.

**Strategy C — Three short auditions.** ~4 minutes with three readers at floor rates. We recommend this only if your goal is learning the platform rather than answering a question — 4-minute sessions end just as the reading gets substantive.

Whichever you choose: the question matters more than the strategy. One specific, written-down question — "what is actually happening between me and [name] since the silence started," not "will I find love" — is the difference between a reading and a horoscope, at any price. ## Five Rules for a First Reading That Is Not Wasted

1. **Research free, spend once.** Journeys and video bios cost nothing. Do the ten-minute protocol, shortlist two readers from this list, then spend the credit on the one whose evidence survives your own audit.
2. **Write the question before connecting.** One sentence, specific, with a first name if a person is involved. Vague questions produce horoscopes at any rate.
3. **Chat first, video later.** Chat is cheaper and produces a transcript you will re-read in three weeks to check the prediction. Video is an upgrade you buy after the reader earns it.
4. **Set the session boundary yourself.** Decide before connecting: "I am spending the credit and nothing more today" or "I will extend to $50 if the first five minutes are specific." The worst first readings are the ones where the budget gets decided mid-session by adrenaline.
5. **Judge in three weeks, not in the moment.** Note the specific, checkable claims — dates, descriptions, "he reaches out by" — and check them when the window closes. That check is how you find your reader for the long term.
