The most common question I receive about psychic readings is also the one most review sites dodge: what does this actually cost? The dodge is understandable — the answer is genuinely complicated, because rates vary by platform, modality, reader experience, and demand, and because the advertised price is almost never the price you will pay. The per-minute rate is the headline; the session structure is the real cost.

Here is the honest, complete picture of what psychic readings cost in 2026, based on years of testing the major platforms, plus the hidden costs and pricing traps that most first-timers discover the hard way.

## The Real Per-Minute Range in 2026

Across the platforms I have tested — [Keen](/reviews/keen/), [Kasamba](/reviews/kasamba/), and [Purple Garden](/reviews/purple-garden/) — per-minute rates fall into three consistent tiers.

Entry-level readers charge roughly $1 to $5 per minute. These are newer advisors building their review counts, and quality varies dramatically within the tier. Some of the best-value readers in the industry sit here; so do most of the worst. On chat modalities, Purple Garden's entry tier can dip below $1 per minute.

Professional readers with established track records charge roughly $5 to $15 per minute. This is where the bulk of good readings live. These readers have thousands of sessions, verifiable review histories, and rates high enough to signal seriousness without the celebrity markup.

Elite specialists charge $15 to $30+ per minute. These are the platform veterans with massive followings — the $20/min Keen readers, the $30+/min Kasamba relationship specialists. The rates are real, and so is the skill, but the only sensible way to use this tier is with a hyper-specific question, which is why the trial offers matter.

The modality shifts the price within the tier. Chat is generally cheapest, phone costs more, and live video costs most, because each format consumes more of the reader's attention. Purple Garden's advisors price each modality separately; on Keen, the rate is set per reader and applies across modalities.

## What the Trial Offers Actually Give You

The advertised offers are real, and they are the smartest money you can spend in this industry — if you understand what they are for.

Keen's 5 minutes for $1 is the best single dollar in psychic services. Used correctly, it is a testing tool: bring one hyper-specific question to the platform's highest-tier readers and see whether they deliver a profound hit in five minutes. An elite reader at $15/min is worth $75 of their time for that dollar.

Kasamba's 3 free minutes with every new advisor is the most powerful vetting system in the industry, because it is not a one-time trial. You can test dozens of advisors for free, and its permanent chat transcripts let you audit every prediction afterward.

Purple Garden's $30 credit on first purchase covers roughly six minutes with a mid-tier reader — enough for a focused single-question video session. Combined with its mandatory video intro bios, it is the lowest-risk first purchase in the industry.

The one thing to know about every trial: it ends. After the offer, standard rates apply, and some platforms require a minimum balance to book premium readers. Budget for the real session, not the trial.

## The Hidden Costs Nobody Tells You About

Per-minute rates tell you less than you think, because the structure of a reading determines the real cost. Three patterns inflate the actual price of every session.

The first is the slow reader. A $2/min reader who burns ten minutes on greetings, clarifications, and spiritual pleasantries costs you the same as a $5/min reader who delivers a complete reading in four minutes. Price per minute is irrelevant; value per minute is everything. This is why I recommend testing reading cadence early in any session — a habit I cover in my [guide on preparing for a reading](/guides/how-to-prepare-for-psychic-reading/).

The second is the session-structure cost. A genuine mediumship session or a complex relationship reading is rarely a five-minute affair. Realistic sessions run 15 to 30 minutes, which means the true cost of a "cheap" reading is often $50 to $150 before you factor anything else. Mediumship and deep relationship work are the most expensive categories in practice, regardless of the per-minute rate.

The third is the escalation pattern. The predatory side of this industry makes its money not on the first session but on the follow-ups: the reader who senses a "blockage," the one who needs a paid ritual, the one who gently requires another session to complete what the last one started. My [guide on spotting fake psychics](/guides/how-to-spot-fake-psychic/) names these patterns explicitly, and the rule is simple — any reader who tries to sell you the next session before the current one has proven itself is pricing you, not reading you.

## Real Session Costs, Platform by Platform

Putting it together with real numbers, based on my actual testing:

A focused 20-minute chat session with a professional-tier reader on Kasamba ($5→–$10/min) runs $100 to $200, minus free minutes. A 20-minute phone session with a professional reader on Keen ($5→–$15/min) runs $100 to $300, with the $1 trial covering the first five minutes. A 20-minute video session on Purple Garden ($5→–$6/min) runs $100 to $120, with the $30 credit cutting the first one to roughly $70 to $90.

The cheapest realistic full reading is around $50; the most expensive are $300+. Anyone who promises you a complete, meaningful reading for $5 is selling you either a trial, a cold read, or a teaser for an escalation.

## How to Budget So You Are Not Burned

Budget for a session the way you would budget for a meal you cannot return: decide the ceiling before you book, tell the reader the ceiling, and structure your question around the minutes you have. A 10-minute session with one surgical question is more valuable than a 30-minute session with a wandering one.

Use the trial offers as intended — testing, not reading. Keep your first real session in the professional tier rather than the elite tier. And audit: for chat readings, keep the transcript and check the predictions when the windows pass. That habit alone is worth more than any pricing comparison, because it tells you which readers are worth the rate they charge.

## The Bottom Line

Psychic readings in 2026 cost between $1 and $30+ per minute, with real sessions landing between $50 and $300 depending on platform, modality, and reader tier. The advertised rate is the least informative number in the industry; what matters is the value delivered per minute, the session structure, and whether the reader survives your audit.

Start with a trial offer, test with a specific question, and escalate only when a reader has earned it.
