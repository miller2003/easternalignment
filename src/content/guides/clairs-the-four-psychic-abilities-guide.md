The word "psychic" suggests a single uniform ability, which is why the industry's language is so unhelpful to beginners. There is no such thing as being psychic in general. There are specific channels through which intuitive information arrives, and they are as distinct as the five physical senses they mirror. A reader who is extraordinary in one channel can be ordinary in another, and the channel shapes everything about how a session feels — which is exactly why knowing the four clairs is the fastest shortcut to choosing the right reader for you.

The four clairs are the industry's operating system. Here is what each one is, how it feels from the inside, how to recognize your own dominant channel, and what it means for the readings you book.

## Clairvoyance: Clear Seeing

Clairvoyance is psychic perception through images. A clairvoyant reader receives information as pictures — a scene, a symbol, a face, a flash of color, a movie-like sequence — and translates those images into the reading. The perception is visual in the way a memory or a daydream is visual, not in the way the physical eye sees.

Clairvoyants often describe the experience as receiving stills or short clips that they must interpret: seeing a kitchen that belongs to the person in your question, an image of a specific street, a symbol like a key or a door that carries meaning in context. The discipline of a good clairvoyant is honest interpretation — a picture of a ring could mean engagement, a contract, or a reminder of a promise, and the reader's job is to identify which by layering the image against your situation.

If your intuitive experiences arrive as pictures — vivid mental images, symbol-heavy dreams, faces that appear in your mind's eye — you lean clairvoyant. The reader to book is the one who describes visual impressions, because they are speaking your language.

## Clairaudience: Clear Hearing

Clairaudience is psychic perception through sound. A clairaudient reader receives information as words, phrases, music, or sounds — an inner voice that is not their own, a song that surfaces with meaning, a name that arrives with a sudden sense of importance.

The honest clairaudient is careful about how they describe this, because "I hear voices" carries baggage the industry has never quite shed. The useful framing is that the information arrives in an auditory form the reader must parse: a specific phrase that lands with weight, a name that will not leave the mind, a piece of music that surfaces at the moment it matters. [Psychic Medium Chloe](/reviews/purple-garden/psychic-medium-chloe/) is a documented example from my testing — her clairaudient channel produced communication-pattern details she had no other way of knowing.

If your intuitive hits arrive as words or sounds — a phrase that pops into your head and proves relevant, a song that surfaces with unsettling timing, hearing your name called when no one called it — you lean clairaudient.

## Clairsentience: Clear Feeling

Clairsentience is psychic perception through feeling — both emotional and physical. A clairsentient reader receives information as bodily and emotional sensations: they feel the client's grief as a weight in the chest, sense anxiety as a knot, register a person's energy as warmth or cold. It is the channel of the empath, and it is widely considered the most common dominant channel, because most people experience it without ever recognizing it as psychic.

Clairsentients absorb. Walking into a room, they feel its emotional temperature before anyone speaks. Meeting a person, they register their state physically. In a reading, the clairsentient's impressions arrive as "the energy feels heavy around this," "I am sensing a lot of holding back in your throat," "there is warmth when I connect to this person." The physical specificity is the signal — a clairsentient who describes bodily sensations is demonstrating the channel; one who describes everything as general energy is not.

If you feel other people's emotions in your body, if rooms have moods you absorb, if you leave social events exhausted — you are clairsentient. This is the channel that dominates the industry's relationship readings, because it is the channel that reads people.

## Claircognizance: Clear Knowing

Claircognizance is psychic perception through sudden, certain knowledge — the answer arrives fully formed, with no image, sound, or feeling attached. It is the most overlooked channel, because it is the easiest to mistake for a normal thought. The knower simply knows: the name, the outcome, the next step, with a certainty that is qualitatively different from reasoning.

The honest claircognizant describes this as receiving downloads — complete information that arrives without process. "I cannot tell you how I know," they say, "but this situation is not what it appears." The channel is fast and can be startlingly accurate, but it is also the channel most vulnerable to contamination by the reader's own beliefs and wishes, because the knowing arrives without sensory evidence to check it against. The best claircognizants have learned to distinguish the genuine knowing from the familiar mental noise, and that self-distinction is the whole craft.

If you have sudden, certain knowledge that turns out right — you know the answer to something before you have any basis to know it, you walk into situations already knowing how they will resolve — you are claircognizant, and you have probably been dismissing the ability your whole life.

## Why Your Dominant Channel Matters When You Book a Reading

Every reader has a dominant channel, and the match between the reader's channel and your situation is a real factor in session quality.

If your question is about people — a partner's motivations, a family dynamic, a new relationship — a clairsentient reader is the natural fit, because the channel reads people. If your question is about events and timing — a move, a job, a reconciliation window — a clairvoyant or claircognizant reader tends to deliver the concrete specifics you are after. If you are seeking contact with someone who has died, mediumship draws most heavily on clairaudience and clairvoyance, which is why the dedicated mediums in my [Keen reader series](/reviews/keen/) describe their work through those channels.

The practical test when you book: read the profile for the channel. A reader who describes images and visions is clairvoyant; one who describes energy and feelings is clairsentient; one who describes knowing is claircognizant. Match the channel to the question, and you have improved your odds before the session begins.

## The Bottom Line

The four clairs are the operating system of the psychic industry: clairvoyance sees, clairaudience hears, clairsentience feels, and claircognizance knows. Every reader is a specialist in one of these channels, every intuitive experience you have arrives through one of them, and the match between channel and question is one of the most underrated factors in reading quality.

Recognize your own channel — it will tell you both how your intuition works and which readers you will understand instantly.
