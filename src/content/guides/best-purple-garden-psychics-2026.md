Purple Garden is the youngest of the three major psychic platforms, and it did something the older two never attempted: it built **verification infrastructure**. On Kasamba or Keen, accuracy is something you assess after spending — you read the reviews, take the session, and find out. Purple Garden's Journeys feature inverts that sequence: a public, permanent prediction ledger where clients document what an advisor told them, then return weeks or months later to confirm or contradict the outcome. Advisors cannot edit or delete entries. No other platform in the industry exposes its readers to that kind of audit at scale.

## How We Ranked: Four Signals, in Order of Difficulty to Fake

**1. The Journeys ledger.** Not all entries are equal. The informative ones contain a specific, dateable prediction and a client who returned after the window to confirm or contradict. A reader with 20 specific, checkable Journeys predictions at a 70% confirmation rate is more credibly accurate than one with 200 vague positive entries. We counted specificity, not enthusiasm.

**2. Repeat-client behavior.** A reader whose current review feed runs 50%+ repeat clients is surviving on rebookings — the one metric in this industry that cannot be bought. We measured repeat concentration for every candidate.

**3. Negative-review content.** We read the 1–2 star reviews, not just the count. "She told me what I didn't want to hear and I was angry" is often delayed evidence of accuracy. "She kept me on the meter" is a structural disqualifier regardless of rating.

**4. Video bio authenticity.** Before any ranking, we screened recorded video intros: deliberate pacing, visible real tools, bounded claims ("I map the current state of a situation") over guarantees ("I will tell you exactly what happens"). Readers whose bios promise certainty about unfalsifiable claims were excluded.

## Using Purple Garden's Verification Tools Before You Book

## FAQs

### Is Purple Garden better than Kasamba or Keen?

Different, with one structural advantage: the Journeys public ledger makes accuracy verifiable before you spend, which neither Kasamba nor Keen offers at scale. Kasamba's counter-advantage is 3 free minutes with *every* new advisor (better for auditioning many readers cheaply); Keen's is phone-first infrastructure and the $1 first five minutes. If you want to verify before paying, start here. If you want to sample many readers at zero cost, [start with Kasamba](/guides/best-kasamba-psychics-2026/).

### Why are video readings more expensive — and when are they worth it?

Video runs 50–100% above chat for most readers because it is the most labor-intensive format: the reader is on camera, in real time, with no editing. It is worth the premium for two situations: mediumship and tool-based readings (watching a shuffle and draw removes the trust requirement), and emotionally complex sessions where you need to see and be seen. For a single focused question, chat delivers the same information at half the price.

### What do the platform badges actually mean?

Top Rated and Highest Rated reflect sustained client ratings; Top Accuracy and Most Accurate reflect platform-side accuracy tracking including Journeys outcomes; Trending reflects recent booking velocity. Weight the accuracy badges above the popularity ones — Trending measures demand, not correctness.
