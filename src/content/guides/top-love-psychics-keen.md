## How to Spend the Keen $1 Trial on a Love Question

Keen's intro offer — 5 minutes for $1 — is a single deep audition, not a sampler. The protocol that maximizes it:

1. **Write the question before you dial.** One sentence, with a first name. Phone minutes bleed faster than chat minutes; preparation is worth real money here.
2. **Spend the first minute on verification.** Give the name and the skeleton, then let the reader produce. A strong love reader volunteers specifics you did not supply; a weak one spends your intro asking you questions.
3. **Decide at minute four.** If the read is landing, continue into paid time with the same reader — switching now costs you the connection you just verified. If it is not, end the call at $1 and the lesson cost you a dollar.
4. **Phone or chat, chosen deliberately.** Keen's top love readers are phone-first — but if you want a transcript to check in three weeks, book a chat-format reader (Kelly777 on this list) or request chat availability on the profile.
