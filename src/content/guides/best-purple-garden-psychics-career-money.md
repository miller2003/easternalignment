## First: Frame the Career Question Correctly

The framing protocol matters more in career readings than anywhere else, because the stakes are concrete and the meter is running. Three rules before you book:

**Ask about the situation, not for the verdict.** "What is actually happening inside this negotiation — what is the other side optimizing for" produces a usable read. "Will I get the job" produces a coin flip with your anxiety attached. The first kind of answer changes what you do Monday morning; the second just changes how you sleep.

**Bring the structure, not the story.** Offer amount, deadline, the players involved, the one thing you cannot figure out. Career readers work best on skeletons: facts they can read the energy around. The narrative of how you feel about it is yours to manage, not theirs to bill for.

**One decision per session.** Every strong reader on this list enforces some version of this rule, and they are right to. A session that tries to cover the job, the side business, and the relocation produces three shallow reads at full price.

## FAQs

### Can a psychic help with a job decision, or is that what mentors are for?

Different inputs, same decision. A mentor reads the industry's visible structure; a career psychic reads the invisible one — the hiring manager's actual inclinations, the trajectory of the situation you are inside, the timing window. The strongest play is not either/or: use the reading for the information you cannot otherwise get, then execute with normal tools. The readers on this list who hold coaching credentials (Sagest, Adam Africa) are built precisely for clients who want both layers in one session.

### Should I book video for a career reading?

Usually no — and that is not a hedge. Career reads are information-dense Q&A; chat delivers the same content at half the price and leaves you a transcript to review before the decision. Book video only if your career situation is emotionally tangled enough that you need the reader to see you — a layoff, a betrayal at work, a partnership breaking. For those, the format earns its premium.

### What is the biggest mistake people make in career readings?

Asking for the verdict instead of the read. "Will I get the job" buys a binary guess. "What is actually happening in this process — what are they optimizing for, and where do I actually stand" buys information you can act on whether the answer is yes or no. Every reader on this list does better work with the second kind of question, and your money does too.

### How do I verify a career prediction afterward?

Career outcomes are among the most checkable predictions a reader can make — the offer arrives or it does not, the deal closes or it does not, the timing window happens or it does not. Save the chat transcript, note the specific claims with dates, and check them at the deadline.
