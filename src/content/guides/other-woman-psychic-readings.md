## The "Dimensional Strike": The Architecture of the Affair

Before you spend money asking if he will choose you, you must understand how a master psychic views this dynamic.

### 1. Are You the "Soulmate" or the "Catalyst"?
Many women in affairs genuinely believe they are Twin Flames, destined to rescue the man from a toxic, dead marriage. An elite psychic will differentiate between two very different karmic roles. Sometimes you are indeed the soulmate he is meant to end up with. But very often, you are simply the **"Catalyst."** The Catalyst is a temporary karmic partner whose only purpose is to give the man the courage to finally blow up his dead marriage. Once the divorce happens, the Catalyst's job is done, and the man moves on to someone else entirely. You must know which role you are playing.

### 2. Validating His Narrative
As the other woman, you only hear his side of the story. You only hear how terrible his wife is and how dead the marriage is. A psychic can tap into the wife's energy (or the energy of their household) and tell you if he is lying. If he claims they haven't slept together in years, but the Tarot cards show the "Lovers" and "Ten of Cups" firmly rooted in his home life, you are being manipulated.

### 3. The Logistical Terror of Divorce
Divorce is not just an emotional event; it is a financial and legal massacre. A psychic like David7 will look at the man's energetic bandwidth. Even if he loves you deeply, does he actually have the financial courage to lose half his assets, pay alimony, and face the anger of his children? If his "courage" energy is blocked, he will never leave.
