## 1. The Dimensional Strike: What a Real Divorce Reading Looks Like

Before you spend a dollar, you must understand what makes a divorce reading structurally different from a standard love reading. 

### A. Breakups are Emotional; Divorces are Logistical
A standard breakup requires an **Empath (Clairsentient)** who can read why your ex pulled away and what they are feeling. A divorce requires a **Clairvoyant (Vision-based) or Astrologer** who can see *events and timelines*. When lawyers and assets are involved, the energy is heavy and dense. You need a reader who can predict legal delays, communication windows, and the presence of hidden third parties.

### B. The "No-Contact" Timeline Fallacy
Fake psychics love to throw out random dates: *"He will text you on the 15th."* Elite psychics understand that "No Contact" is an energetic reset. Instead of giving you a fixed date that causes you anxiety, they will map a rolling timeline: *"His avoidant energy will peak in four weeks, after which the communication block will lift, provided you do not break the silence first."*

### C. The "Return Spell" Scam Warning
If any psychic tells you that your ex is under a black magic spell, or that they can cast a "return spell" to bring them back for an extra fee, **hang up immediately and report their profile.** Real psychics read trajectories; they do not manipulate free will for cash.

## How to Prepare for a Divorce Psychic Reading

When you are paying by the minute, you cannot afford to waste time venting. Follow these rules to get the most accurate diagnostic:

**1. State the Legal Reality Upfront:**
Do not play "test the psychic." Start the session by saying: *"My husband filed for divorce on Tuesday. I need you to look at his current energetic trajectory and tell me if this is a permanent break or a temporary panic."* This allows the psychic to skip the guesswork and start deep-diving immediately.

**2. Ask for the "What Now," Not Just the "What If":**
Instead of asking, *"Will he cancel the divorce?"* (which surrenders all your power), ask, *"What is the most energetically favorable path for me to take right now regarding these legal proceedings?"* This forces the reading to focus on your empowerment and protection.
