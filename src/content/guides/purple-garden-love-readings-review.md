## 1. Seeing the Spread: Why Video Tarot Beats Text Chat

In a standard text chat reading, an advisor can type *"The Lovers card just appeared"* without ever shuffling a deck. On Purple Garden's Live Video channel, visual accountability reigns:

## 2. Breaking the "No Contact" Waiting Trap

One of the most damaging patterns in psychic advice is the endless loop of *"He miss you and will text next week."*
