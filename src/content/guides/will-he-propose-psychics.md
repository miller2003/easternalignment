## The "Dimensional Strike": How Psychics Actually Predict Proposals

Before you book a session, you must understand how a real psychic views the architecture of commitment.

### 1. Commitment Phobia vs. Lack of Love
The biggest mistake clients make is assuming that if he hasn't proposed, he doesn't love you. In esoteric readings, these are two completely different energetic signatures. A deep psychic will often reveal that his delay has nothing to do with you. It is often rooted in a hidden financial insecurity (he feels he hasn't "built his kingdom" yet) or inherited trauma (a deep subconscious fear of repeating his parents' divorce). Once you understand the *why*, the timeline becomes clear.

### 2. The 7th House Transit (Astrological Timing)
A proposal is not a random event; it is an energetic milestone. Advanced readers (like Arradaza) do not guess. They use Astrological Synastry to calculate exactly when Jupiter or Venus will cross your 7th House of Marriage. This gives them a mathematical, astronomical window (e.g., *"between October 15th and November 30th"*) during which the proposal energy will peak.

### 3. The Danger of the "Energetic Push"
If a psychic tells you the proposal window is six months away, and you decide to issue an ultimatum today out of impatience, you will destroy the timeline. True psychics map the trajectory, but they also warn you *when to back off* so the masculine energy can step forward naturally.
