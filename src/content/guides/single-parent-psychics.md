Before you introduce a man to your children, you must ensure your psychic is reading the complete energetic picture. ### 1. The "Household Aura" Integration
A household is a closed energetic system. You and your children have established a baseline frequency. When a new man enters the house, his aura crashes into yours. A true psychic will not just look at your Synastry with him; they will look at his energetic compatibility with *your children*. If his energy is overly domineering, impatient, or chaotic, an empathic psychic will see the children's auras shrinking to protect themselves before he ever raises his voice.

### 2. Translating the Child's Rejection
When a child says, *"I hate him,"* they rarely mean it literally. They lack the emotional vocabulary to express complex fears. They can tell you: *"Your son doesn't hate him; your son is terrified that if you love this new man, you won't have enough love left for him."* Knowing the *why* changes your entire parenting strategy.

### 3. The 4th House and the 5th House
In Astrology, the 5th House rules Children, and the 4th House rules the Home. A structural astrologer will look at your new partner's chart to see how his planets interact with these houses. If his chart shows massive turbulence in the 4th House, it means he is structurally incapable of maintaining a peaceful domestic life, no matter how much he loves you.
