## What a Genuine Reconciliation Reading Actually Looks Like

Most people who book an ex-recovery reading are hoping for a sequence: confirmation that the ex still has feelings, a timeline for contact, instructions for what to do next. A legitimate reconciliation reading on Kasamba delivers something different —and more useful.

**What it tells you:** The current energetic state of the other person in relation to this connection. Whether the trajectory is moving toward or away from reconnection. The specific psychological driver or external factor currently governing their behavior. What, if any, conditions would need to change for the trajectory to shift.

**What it does not tell you:** What the other person will definitively choose. When they will definitely contact you. Whether the reunion is guaranteed. A specific prediction about another person's behavior is a probabilistic read on current trajectory —it can be accurate under current conditions and still prove incorrect if the person makes a fundamentally different choice.

The most useful reconciliation readings I have encountered are ones that answer the question *"what do I need to understand about this situation to make the best decision for myself"* rather than *"confirm the outcome I want."* That reframing changes both what you ask and what you do with the answer.

## The 3-Free-Minutes Protocol for Ex-Recovery Questions

Using Kasamba's free minutes correctly for an ex-recovery question requires a slightly different approach than a general reading.

**Craft your opening message in advance.** Enter it the moment the chat opens. Something like: *"His name is [Name], my name is [Name]. We broke up [X weeks] ago. He has been in no-contact. I want to know his current energetic state —is he still connected to this, and is there movement?"*

**Provide nothing else.** Do not mention who initiated the breakup, what was said, whether there was a third party, or what you have been doing since the breakup. The reader should generate this information, not receive it from you.

**Watch for specificity that you did not provide.** A genuinely connected reader will deliver characteristically specific information about the person —something that sounds like them, describes their likely behavior or thought-patterns, or references a dynamic of the relationship that you recognize as accurate without having stated it. This is the vetting signal.

**The vetting question.** If the reader provides something plausible but vague in the first 90 seconds, use the remaining free time to ask: *"Can you be more specific about what his energy feels like right now? Is it moving toward or away from this?"* A reader genuinely connected to the situation will be able to specify. A reader cold-reading from generic patterns will continue to generalize.

**If nothing specific emerges, exit cleanly.** The free minutes expire, you paid nothing. The next reader on your list gets the same opening. Over three or four attempts, you will identify the reader who is actually connecting to the specific energy of your situation rather than delivering the reconciliation-optimistic framework that most clients in this situation want to hear.

## FAQs

### Can a Kasamba psychic actually tell me if my ex is coming back?

A skilled empathic or telepathic reader can assess the current energetic trajectory of the connection and your ex's current emotional state relative to it. This is meaningfully different from predicting a guaranteed outcome: trajectory reads describe where the momentum is heading under current conditions, not what another person will definitively choose. A reader who claims certainty about another person's future choices is selling you something beyond their actual capability.

### Is Kasamba's chat format better than a phone reading for ex-recovery questions?

For most ex-recovery questions, yes. The chat format eliminates the auditory cold-reading vulnerability that is particularly dangerous when you are emotionally distressed about a specific person. It also produces a permanent transcript that you can audit against what actually happens —which is uniquely valuable for reconciliation timeline predictions. The phone format has advantages for certain types of clairvoyant readers; the chat format has structural advantages specifically for the high-emotional-stakes question category.

### How many sessions should I book on this question?

One high-quality session with a verified reader should produce a complete enough picture to guide your next decision. The dangerous pattern is booking multiple sessions in rapid succession when the first answer is uncomfortable —this is sometimes called "psychic hopping" and it is the single most expensive behavior in the industry. Book one session with the most verified reader you can access via the free minutes, sit with the answer for a minimum of one week, and evaluate it against what you observe in reality before considering another session.

### What do I do if the reading says the ex is not coming back?

First, sit with the information rather than immediately dismissing it or booking another reader who might give a different answer. Second, re-read the transcript 48 hours later when the emotional charge has reduced —information that felt devastating in the moment often reads differently, more structurally useful, at lower emotional temperature. Third, consider what action you can take that serves your wellbeing regardless of the reconciliation outcome. The most valuable ex-recovery readings are ones that tell you what to do with yourself, not just what to wait for.

### What is the difference between an ex-energy read and a reunion timeline prediction?

An ex-energy read describes the current state of the other person's feelings and thoughts in relation to you —what is present for them right now. A reunion timeline prediction describes a future event and when it is likely to occur. The first is grounded in current energetic reality and can be verified against subsequent events relatively quickly. The second is a probabilistic projection of momentum and is more difficult to verify and more susceptible to interpretation. The readers on this list are strongest for ex-energy reads; reunion timeline predictions are a secondary output of the same skill.
