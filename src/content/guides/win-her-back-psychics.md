## The "Dimensional Strike": Why Men Fail the Breakup

Before you book a session to ask how to get her back, you must understand why your current strategy is pushing her further away.

### 1. The "Ick" Factor vs. The "Provider" Instinct
When an ex-girlfriend pulls away, the male instinct is to double down on "providing." Men will buy flowers, offer to pay her bills, or show up at her house to "fix" things. In the energetic realm, this is suffocating. It triggers the female "ick"—the sudden loss of attraction caused by desperation. A master psychic will teach you how to pull your energy completely back, forcing her to lean forward.

### 2. Translating "Mixed Signals"
Men are logical; women are emotional. If she says, *"I just need space right now,"* the logical male brain assumes she hates him. An elite Empath will bypass her words and read the underlying energy. Often, *"I need space"* actually means, *"I am overwhelmed by your anxiety and need you to prove you can stand on your own two feet before I come back."*

### 3. The Re-Engagement Window
You cannot force communication. Astrology and Tarot are exceptional at identifying "Re-Engagement Windows." A psychic can tell you, *"Do not text her this week. Her energy is totally blocked. She will enter a period of deep nostalgia next Thursday—that is when you send the text."*

## How Men Should Talk to a Psychic

If you want an accurate reading that actually results in getting her back, you must drop the masculine ego.

**1. Stop Trying to Prove You Were Right**
Do not spend your paid minutes trying to convince the psychic that your ex-girlfriend was crazy or that the breakup wasn't your fault. The psychic does not care. They are there to read the energy, not be your lawyer.

**2. Ask Strategic Questions**
Do not ask, *"Does she still love me?"* (Love does not equal reconciliation).
Ask: *"What is the primary energetic blockage keeping her from returning, and what specific action (or inaction) do I need to take over the next 30 days to clear it?"*
