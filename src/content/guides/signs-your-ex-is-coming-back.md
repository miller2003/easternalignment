"Is my ex coming back" is the question the psychic industry is built on. It is the first question a third of all clients ask, the question that keeps the phone lines busy at 2 AM, and the question that produces both the industry's most meaningful work and its most cynical exploitation. I have reviewed dozens of readers who answer it, and I have developed a clear picture of what the honest ones look for — and what the dishonest ones sell instead.

Here is the truth about reconciliation readings: the signs are real, but they are signs of possibility, not guarantees. The value of a good reading is not a yes-or-no verdict. It is an honest assessment of whether the connection is in a pause or a close — and the discipline to tell you which one you are hoping for.

## The Signs Readers Actually Evaluate

When a competent reader assesses reconciliation, they are not reading tea leaves; they are evaluating a specific set of signals about the state of the connection. Through my testing and interviews, the honest readers consistently weigh the same factors.

**Lingering energetic attachment.** The readers describe this as a cord or thread that remains intact after a breakup. Its presence does not mean reunion; it means unfinished business — which can resolve toward reconciliation, toward closure, or toward a final painful severing. The useful information is not whether the thread exists, but what it is attached to.

**Unclosed threads and unresolved practical ties.** The most concrete signal, and the one most grounded in reality: shared leases, joint accounts, unreturned belongings, unresolved conversations. Where practical entanglement remains, the door is rarely fully closed. Where everything was cleanly severed, the opposite is usually true.

**Sustained contact patterns.** Not the occasional like, but actual initiative — reaching out, asking questions, referencing the relationship, checking in with no functional purpose. The honest readers distinguish between contact and interest, because people sometimes reach out for closure, guilt, or habit rather than return.

**The difference between an ending and a pause.** This is the core judgment. A breakup that happens because two people cannot stop fighting has a different trajectory from one that happens because of timing, external pressure, or a specific crisis. The best readers assess which category your breakup falls into, and that assessment matters more than any single sign.

## The False Hope Traps

The reason reconciliation is the most exploitable category in the industry is that false hope is indistinguishable from real hope at the moment you are carrying it. The scammers know this. They build their entire business on it.

The classic pattern is the waiting loop: the reader confirms he is coming back, gives you a window, the window passes, and you book again to ask why. The answer is always the same — timing shifted, energy blocked, needs another session — and the fee is always due. This is the single most profitable scam pattern in the industry, and it is built entirely on the client's unwillingness to stop believing. My [guide on spotting fake psychics](/guides/how-to-spot-fake-psychic/) names it explicitly, and my [guide on the no-contact protocol](/guides/no-contact-psychic-readings-guide/) explains why the waiting loop flourishes during enforced silence.

The other trap is projection masquerading as reading. A reader tells you what you want to hear — the reunion, the timeline, the certainty — and you accept it because it matches your hope. The honest readers in my testing refused this repeatedly. The signal that separates them: they tell you when they see finality. A reader who can tell you "this one is over" is a reader whose "this one is coming back" means something.

## The Test for Real Signals

You can apply the same framework the honest readers use, without spending a dollar. The test for whether a sign is real is whether it is anchored in your ex's behavior or in your interpretation of it.

Behavior is real: initiative, contact, unresolved practical ties, a stated or demonstrated change in the pattern that ended the relationship. Interpretation is projection: the like on an old photo, the song they posted, the "coincidence" of running into their friend. The difference between the two is the difference between reading the situation and reading your own wishes. If you are honest with yourself about which column most of your "signs" fall into, you will already know what the honest readers would tell you.

The timing question matters too. Reconciliation energy, when it exists, has a shape: it either consolidates within a reasonable window or it dissipates. A reader who gives you a specific window and a mechanism ("he will reach out within two weeks, driven by the unresolved lease") is giving you something testable. A reader who gives you an open-ended "it will happen when the timing is right" is giving you a subscription.

## What to Ask in a Reading (and What Not to Ask)

If you book a reconciliation reading, the questions that produce honest answers are the ones that do not force a verdict.

Ask: "Is this connection in a pause or a close?" — the single best framing, because it asks for a read on the state rather than a prediction. "What is the energy of my ex toward this relationship right now?" "What would reconciliation require, and is it present?" "What is my part in this pattern, regardless of the outcome?"

Do not ask: "Is he coming back, yes or no?" — this forces the guess. And never accept a reader who tells you to keep waiting without a testable window and mechanism. My [guide on questions to ask a psychic](/guides/questions-to-ask-a-psychic/) has the full framework.

## The Honest Bottom Line

The signs that an ex is coming back are real, and the honest readers evaluate them the same way a competent friend would — attachment, unresolved ties, contact patterns, and the difference between a pause and a close. The dishonest readers sell certainty, windows without mechanisms, and the waiting loop that converts your hope into recurring payments.

The most valuable thing a reconciliation reading can give you is not a verdict. It is an honest read on whether you are in a pause or a close — because the answer determines what you do with the next season of your life, and no amount of hoping changes which one it is.
