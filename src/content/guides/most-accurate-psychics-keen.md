## How We Define and Measure Accuracy on Keen

Before the list, the framework — because "accurate" is not a single dimension.

### Timeline Accuracy

Timeline accuracy is the ability to correctly predict when a specific event will occur: when contact will resume, when a relationship will progress to a specific stage, when an external circumstance will shift. These are the most falsifiable predictions a psychic can make, which means a reader with a strong track record for timeline accuracy has demonstrated something that cannot be sustained through luck alone.

Keen's phone-dominant architecture has a structural advantage for this accuracy type: voice-native clairvoyants who work through auditory perception can often access timeline information with more immediacy than chat-based readers. The voice carries energetic frequency; a skilled clairaudient working in their optimal modality is performing at a higher level than the same reader forced into a text environment.

The review pattern for genuine timeline accuracy: clients who report specific predictions that materialized within the stated window, combined with clients whose negative reviews describe failed specific predictions (indicating the reader made testable claims at all).

### Diagnostic and Pattern Accuracy

Diagnostic accuracy is the ability to describe the current psychological state, hidden motivations, behavioral patterns, or energetic dynamics of a situation or person — without being told. This is the "why" category: why is this person behaving this way, what is the emotional state beneath the behavior, what pattern in the dynamic is producing the current outcome.

This type of accuracy is most verifiable through the quality of the negative reviews. A reader whose 1–2 star reviews cite "she told me things I didn't want to hear" or "she described a painful truth I wasn't ready to accept" is likely a high-diagnostic-accuracy reader. A reader whose negative reviews cite "she kept saying things that weren't quite right" or "I felt like she was just asking questions" is indicating methodological problems.

### Emotional Resonance Accuracy

Emotional resonance accuracy is the ability to describe the felt-state of another person — what they are actually experiencing emotionally, as distinct from what they are expressing or what the seeker believes they feel. This is the most subjective of the three accuracy types and the hardest to verify from outside a session. But for readers with very high written review rates — clients who are so moved by a session that they type out paragraphs of explanation — the qualitative content of those reviews provides indirect evidence of this accuracy type.

## FAQs

### How do I know if a Keen psychic is accurate before paying?

Use Keen's un-deletable review system for longitudinal pattern analysis. Look at the ratio of written reviews to total ratings (higher = more emotionally resonant sessions), the type of complaints in negative reviews (accuracy failures vs. uncomfortable accurate truths), and the session volume over time (sustained high volume eliminates luck as an explanation). Then use the $1 trial to test the reader directly before committing to a full session.

### Is the most expensive Keen reader the most accurate?

Not necessarily. Rate is a function of multiple factors including demand management and brand positioning, not accuracy alone. David7 is among the most accurate readers on the platform for his specialty; his rate is in the mid-premium range. Tarot With Meg offers exceptional Hellenistic timing accuracy at rates that do not reflect the full premium of her methodology's complexity. Always match the reader to your question type rather than assuming rate = quality.

### Can I get an accurate Keen reading via chat instead of phone?

Yes, though some accuracy types are better expressed via phone. Arradaza and Tarot With Meg's astrological methodologies translate well to chat — the calculation is the same regardless of format. David7 and Flora Knows All's empathic methodologies are enhanced by the voice channel. Master Sher's Tarot readings can be delivered via chat with the card names and positions typed explicitly — producing an accountability record similar to Kasamba's chat transcript.
