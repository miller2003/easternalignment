I went into my first psychic reading having done almost no preparation. I had a rough idea of what I wanted to know —something about a relationship I was confused about —and I had a vague plan to "just see what comes up."

The result was a reading that felt mildly interesting in the moment and completely inert in retrospect. The reader said things that were true in the way that things said to anyone in a state of romantic uncertainty tend to be true. I spent forty-one dollars and left with nothing I could hold onto.

My second reading, with different preparation and a completely different approach, was worth more than a year of journaling on the same subject.

The difference was not the reader. It was me.

This is a guide about how to be the second version of yourself before you walk into your first session.

### How to Select an Individual Reader

Within whichever platform you choose, reader selection is where most first-timers make mistakes. They either choose someone based on the profile photo, pick the first five-star rating they see, or simply go with whoever is available.

A more systematic approach:

**Step 1: Filter by specialization, not by general rating.** If your question is about a relationship, search specifically for readers who list love, relationships, or specific-person readings in their specialization. A generalist with a 4.9 rating may be significantly less useful for your specific question than a relationship specialist with a 4.7.

**Step 2: Read the negative reviews as much as the positive ones.** Five-star reviews tell you the reader can deliver. One and two-star reviews tell you what they look like when they do not. Look for negative reviews that criticize vagueness or inaccuracy (legitimate feedback) rather than negative reviews that complain the reader told the client something they did not want to hear (often a sign of reader integrity).

**Step 3: Look for readers who ask for birth information.** In a love reading, a reader who asks for names and birth dates before beginning has a structured, data-based approach. A reader who asks "so, what's been going on?" is asking you to do their job for them.

## Preparing Your Question

This step is where most first-timers dramatically underinvest. The quality of your question is the single largest determinant of the quality of your answer.

### The Wrong Way to Ask a Psychic Question

These question formats will produce minimal value:

- *"Can you tell me what you see around me?"* —too open, generates generalities
- *"What does my future look like?"* —no scope, no traction, produces vague imagery
- *"Will things work out?"* —binary and unanswerable with precision
- *"What do you think I should do about my situation?"* —the reader does not know your situation, so they will ask you to describe it, and then reflect your own analysis back at you

### The Right Way to Ask a Psychic Question

Effective psychic questions are:

**Specific about the subject.** Not "a relationship" —the name, the birth date, the nature of the connection.

**Diagnostic rather than predictive.** Not "will he come back?" but "what is his current emotional state regarding our connection, and what trajectory is he on?"

**Open rather than binary.** Not "does she love me?" but "what is the quality and depth of her feeling for me, and what is blocking its expression?"

**Anchored in the present moment.** Psychic perception is most precise at reading current energetic states. Questions about the present state of someone's feelings are more reliably answered than questions about specific future dates.

### Write It Down Before You Connect

Your question should be written out, in precise language, before you initiate the session. This serves two purposes: it forces clarity in your own mind, and it allows you to paste or state your question immediately when the session begins —before any anxiety or the reader's opening lines can throw you off.

Bring a maximum of two to three questions, ranked by priority. Start with the most important one.

## The First Three Minutes: The Most Critical Window

Whether you are using Kasamba's free minutes or Keen's introductory rate, the first three minutes of a reading are where you assess whether this reader is worth continuing with.

Here is what you are watching for:

### Signs You Have a Legitimate Reader

**They lead with perception, not questions.** In the first minute, a genuine reader should be providing something —an impression, an observation, a feeling —not asking you to explain your situation. If the first minute is primarily them asking you questions, they are gathering information to mirror back.

**They are specific without prompting.** The benchmark of genuine perception is specificity you did not provide. Not *"I sense there is tension in this relationship"* (true of almost every relationship question) but *"what I'm picking up on his side is a specific kind of guilt —not about what happened between you, but about something he knows he has been avoiding that is outside the relationship itself."* Specific, unsolicited, particular.

**They are willing to say something uncomfortable.** A practitioner who is reading energy rather than reading your emotional needs will occasionally tell you something you would rather not hear. This is not cruelty —it is professionalism. If everything in the first three minutes sounds like validation and affirmation, proceed with caution.

### Signs You Should Disconnect Without Paying

On Kasamba, you can disconnect before your three free minutes expire and pay nothing. Do this immediately if the reader:

- Has spent more than 90 seconds asking you questions rather than providing impressions
- Makes a claim about a "curse," "dark energy," or "spiritual blockage" that requires additional paid work to resolve
- Provides statements so generic they could apply to any person in any situation
- Asks leading questions that prompt you to validate their guesses ("I'm sensing something difficult happened recently —is that right?")

You are not being rude. You are being a careful consumer of a service. A legitimate practitioner will not take offense at a client who exercises appropriate skepticism.

## During the Reading: How to Participate Effectively

### The Minimum Information Protocol

During the actual session, your job is to confirm or deny specific perceptions —not to explain and narrate. When a reader says something, respond with:

- "Yes, that's accurate" or "No, that doesn't resonate" —without elaborating
- Ask a follow-up question based on what they said, not based on what you want to say next

The principle: a legitimate reader should be able to build the picture. Your role is to verify accuracy and redirect where needed, not to co-construct the narrative.

### When to Offer Clarification

There are moments where offering context is appropriate and useful. If a reader is clearly picking up on a real element but slightly mislocating it —the energy they are describing is accurate but they have attributed it to the wrong time period, or the right dynamic but the wrong person —a brief correction helps them calibrate without handing them the entire story.

The test: are you correcting a small error in an otherwise accurate picture? Or are you providing the information they should have perceived independently? If the latter, stop.

### Taking Notes

In a Kasamba chat reading, transcripts are saved automatically —review them later when you are calmer. In a Keen phone reading, have a notepad or your phone's notes app ready. You will not remember everything in the state of emotional activation that often accompanies these sessions. The details that seem confusing in the moment frequently become precise in retrospect.

## After the Reading: Evaluating What You Received

A first reading should leave you with at least one of the following:

**A specific perception you did not provide, which is verifiable.** Something the reader said about the other person, the history, or the current situation that you can now confirm was accurate —without having given them that information. This is the clearest evidence of genuine perception.

**A reframe that makes something previously confusing make sense.** Not a prediction, but a shift in how you understand the dynamic —an explanation of behavior that was previously opaque, or a naming of a pattern that you recognized the moment it was articulated.

**A clear question for your next step.** A good reading rarely provides all the answers. It provides the most important questions —the ones you need to sit with. If you left with a question you did not walk in with, and it is a genuinely useful one, the reading delivered value.

### What to Do With a Reading That Did Not Resonate

Not every reading will hit. A reader may simply not connect with your specific energy, the timing may not be right, or you may have encountered one of the mediocre practitioners who exist on every platform.

Do not draw broad conclusions from a single poor reading. Use the vetting system —particularly Kasamba's free-minutes-per-reader structure —to try two or three readers before settling on one. A first session that did not connect is information: you now know what an unconnected reading feels like, which makes recognizing a real one easier.

## FAQs

### Should I tell the reader I am skeptical?

Yes, and early. A legitimate reader is not threatened by skepticism —they welcome it, because it raises the standard they are being held to and makes your validation more meaningful. An unethical reader will try to use your skepticism against you, claiming it blocks the energy or requires you to "open up" before they can read accurately. That claim is the read itself: disconnect.

### What if the reader asks for my birth time —is that a red flag?

Not at all. An astrologer-psychic asking for your exact birth time is doing legitimate astrological work and will use it to calculate your Rising sign and house placements. This is appropriate. What would be a red flag is a reader asking for your bank account or social security number for any reason whatsoever —that is never a legitimate request.

### How soon after an event should I get a reading?

Energy is most readable in a state of active movement. Immediately after a significant event —a breakup, a major fight, a sudden silence —the energetic field is dynamic and a skilled reader can perceive it clearly. During periods of static stability, readings are often less eventful. Trust your intuition about when something is worth looking at.
