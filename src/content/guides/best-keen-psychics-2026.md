## How to Spend the Keen 5-Minutes-for-$1 Offer

Keen's intro offer is a single deep audition, not a sampler. The protocol:

1. **Write the question before you dial.** One sentence, with a first name. Phone minutes bleed faster than chat minutes — preparation is money.
2. **Spend the first minute on verification.** Give the name and the skeleton, then let the reader produce. A strong reader volunteers specifics you did not supply; a weak one spends your intro asking you questions.
3. **Decide at minute four.** If the read is landing, continue into paid time — switching now costs the connection you just verified. If it is not, end at $1 and the lesson cost a dollar.
4. **Choose phone or chat deliberately.** Keen's top bench is phone-first, but if you want a transcript to check in three weeks, book a chat-format reader (Kelly777 on this list) or request chat on the profile.
