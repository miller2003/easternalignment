At $9.99/min, the cost of finding out the wrong way is higher than with almost any other advisor on the platform, so calibration before you call matters more here than usual.

Mike Pace joined Keen in 2001. He currently carries roughly **81,000 ratings at 4.95 stars** and is tagged by Keen as a Top Advisor. ### Why a Large Sample at a High Average Beats a Perfect Score

A 5.0 from 200 people tells you almost nothing. A 4.95 from 81,000 people tells you the negative reviews are the exception, not the pattern. If a reader were inconsistent, 81,000 ratings would drag the average down hard. Mike's has not moved. That is the single most useful thing to know before you spend a dollar with him.

## Reading Style in Practice: What 81,000 Ratings Reveal

Mike is tagged **Direct**, not Compassionate. That is a meaningful distinction on Keen. A direct reader fires facts and impressions; a compassionate reader walks you through the situation with reassurance. Clients who want a quick, unambiguous verdict tend to rate direct readers highly. Clients who want to be held through a hard moment often find them abrupt.

Real reviews from his profile repeat a clear pattern:

> "Mike is my favourite and best reader on keen. he has so much patience with me, and always tells me the truth even when I don't want to hear it."

> "He picked up on my situation within the first minute without me explaining anything. Called it exactly."

Two things stand out. First, the word **"patience"** sits next to "direct" - clients describe him as fast *and* willing to stay with a question, which is rare for a direct reader. Second, the unprompted-specificity signal is strong: clients describe him naming dynamics they never supplied. That pattern across tens of thousands of ratings is not coincidental.

### The One Pattern Worth Knowing Before You Call

Because he is direct and fast, sessions can be efficient - but at $9.99/min, efficiency is exactly what protects your budget. Come with a tight question, let him lead, and resist free-associating. You will get the same accuracy in less time, and the difference between a 10-minute and a 20-minute session is nearly $200 at this rate.

## The $9.99/Min Question: A Different Category Entirely

At $9.99/min, Mike is priced in a tier occupied by only a handful of Keen's most established advisors. Whether that premium is justified depends entirely on what you are buying.

- **The $1 intro** gives you 5 minutes. That is your diagnostic window - state your question loosely and see what he picks up unprompted.
- **A real session** - enough time for initial impressions plus a follow-up exchange - typically runs 10-15 minutes, putting the true cost at **$100-$300** at standard rate.
- **The counterpoint:** because he leads rather than waits for your context, you are not paying him to ask you questions. Many clients report he starts talking within seconds.

If you have been burned by readers who echoed your own words back as "insight," Mike's direct, detail-first approach is a genuine contrast. In that specific scenario, the rate buys something different - but it is still a real financial decision.
