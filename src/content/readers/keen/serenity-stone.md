### Why a Medium's Average Runs Lower - and Why That's Not Automatically Bad

Medium sessions are intrinsically harder to score. A love prediction is "right" or "wrong" in a way a spirit message rarely is. Clients evaluating a medium often rate the *comfort* and *resonance* of the experience more than verifiable accuracy. So a 4.66 for a medium can represent a very different client experience than a 4.66 for a prediction-focused reader. Keep that distinction in mind before you weight the number too heavily.
