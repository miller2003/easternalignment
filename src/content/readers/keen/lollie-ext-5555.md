## Before You Book: What 70,000 Readings on Keen Actually Proves

When an advisor shows 70,182 readings on Keen, that is not a number you can manufacture. You survive two decades on a platform this competitive only by retaining a large base of repeat clients who keep coming back. For Lollie, the longevity points to one thing: people who want the truth fast keep her busy.

Lollie joined Keen in 2001. She currently carries roughly **20,000 ratings at 4.92 stars** and bills **$6.99/min**. Keen tags her as Direct, and she leans into that identity harder than almost anyone else on the platform.

### Why a Direct Reader Is a Different Product

On Keen, "Direct" versus "Compassionate" is the single most important filter. A direct reader fires what they see: facts, timelines, hard calls. A compassionate reader wraps the same insight in reassurance. Lollie is unambiguously direct - she literally tells clients to hang up the second they are finished, because she respects that the clock is running. If you have ever resented a reader who stalled to rack up minutes, that posture is a feature, not a bug.

## Reading Style in Practice: What 20,000 Ratings Reveal

Lollie's whole pitch is that backstories and small talk "waste time" and "cloud the reading." She asks for three things up front: your first name, the other person's name, and your first question. Then she goes.

Real reviews from her profile repeat a clear pattern:

> "You can't get more RAW than this. She was honest and she was right. Real deal, accurate."

> "I'm more black coffee than a Frappuccino with whipped cream. I skip asking 'how are you?' because I respect your time."

Two things stand out. First, the word **"honest"** dominates her feedback - clients who want the truth, not a fantasy, rate her highly. Second, the trade-off is real: if you want to be soothed, she will not do it. Multiple reviews note she is "right and raw," which some readers love and others find too blunt.

### The One Pattern Worth Knowing Before You Call

Because she refuses to lead you with questions, the quality of your reading depends almost entirely on the quality of your questions. She tells clients to write questions "in order of priority" so you can "fly right through them." Arrive vague and you will get a shorter, less useful session. Arrive with a tight list and you will get more value per dollar than with almost any chatty reader.

## The $6.99/Min Question: Is She Priced Fairly?

At $6.99/min, Lollie sits in Keen's middle band - well below premium names like Mike Pace ($9.99/min) but above budget readers like Susan 100 (under $3/min).

- **The $1 intro** gives you 5 minutes. State your top question and see what she picks up.
- **Her own "FIRSTREAD" promo** gives 3 free minutes if you message her first (not call/chat) - a useful way to sample her without the intro charge.
- **A real session** - enough for a few prioritized questions plus follow-up - typically runs 10-15 minutes, putting true cost at **$70-$105**.

If you are the kind of client who rambles, a direct reader like Lollie can actually be cheaper than a compassionate one, because she will not let you wander.
