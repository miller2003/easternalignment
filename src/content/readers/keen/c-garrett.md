## The Label Says "Tarot Reader." Her Own Bio Contradicts That in the First Paragraph.

C Garrett is listed under Tarot Card Readings on Keen. Her own profile description makes that categorization immediately complicated: *"75% of my readings come from energies I pick up on and from spirit. The cards help me interpret what I'm feeling if necessary."*

That's not a tarot reader who uses intuition. That's an intuitive who occasionally reaches for cards when she needs to anchor what she's already perceiving. The distinction matters because it changes what you should expect from a session  -  and whether the tarot label is the right reason to book her at all.

### Why the Category She's Listed Under May Be the Least Accurate Thing About Her

Keen's category system pushes readers toward a primary tool label. For C Garrett, that label is Tarot  -  but her listed skills tell a fuller story: Clairvoyant, Empath, Clairsentient, Dream Exploration, Dream Interpretation. The cards are one tool in a much wider repertoire, and by her own account, a secondary one.

If you're searching for someone to do a structured Celtic Cross spread with card-by-card interpretation, she can do that  -  but it's not her native mode. If you want someone whose first move is to read the energy of your situation and reach for a card only when it helps her sharpen what she's already picking up, that's a closer fit.

### What "75% Energy, 25% Cards" Means for How a Session Actually Unfolds

In practice, this hybrid approach produces a reading that feels more like a conversation with someone who already knows things about your situation than a card-driven reveal. She's not building a picture from the spread outward  -  she's already working from energetic perception and using the cards as a cross-reference when something needs more definition.

For clients who find pure card readings too structured or impersonal, this lands well. For clients who specifically want the card framework to hold onto  -  something visual and symbolic they can track  -  it may feel less anchored than they expected.

## "I Am Not a Timing Expert"  -  The Most Honest Line in Her Profile

Carolyn's profile states it plainly: *"I am not a timing expert but will take an honest approach in getting you a time frame for your results."*

### Why Self-Declared Limitations Are a Green Flag

On a platform where readers routinely claim to offer "precise dates and details," a reader who voluntarily tells you where her accuracy thins out is doing something unusual. It takes away a selling point in exchange for being trustworthy. That trade-off is worth noting.

It also tells you something practically useful: she can give you a directional timeframe, and she'll be honest when she can't nail it down precisely. That's more useful than a confident specific date that's actually a guess.

### What This Means for How You Frame Your Questions

Don't open a session with "when will he come back?" or "what date will I hear about the job?" Those questions put her in a position she's told you she's not optimally suited for. Instead: "what's the current state of this situation and what's the likely next movement?" That's a question she can answer with depth and accuracy. The timeframe, when it comes, will be honest  -  approximate rather than false-precise.

But the delivery is direct  -  she'll tell you how your own choices are affecting the situation, which can land harder than a reader who focuses only on external circumstances.

The "too blunt" feedback in her reviews doesn't come from cruelty. It comes from the gap between what a client hoped to hear and what she actually said. For clients who can separate those two things  -  who want the truth even when it stings  -  her style is an asset. ### Who Thrives With Her Communication Style

The clients who return to her repeatedly tend to be people who value being told the truth over being made to feel good temporarily.
