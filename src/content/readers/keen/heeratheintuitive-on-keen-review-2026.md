What she is selling is an ongoing advisory relationship — the kind where a reader who already knows your situation can track its evolution across weeks and months, update her read as circumstances shift, and give you guidance that is calibrated to where things actually stand rather than where they stood when you first called. That model requires context to function. Asking for it upfront is not a limitation — it is a declaration of what kind of service this is. At $7.99/min, she is not pricing a cold-reading test. She is pricing two years of consistent insight, delivered session by session to clients like kristin emily, who wrote: *"I have been talking with Heera for the past two plus years"*. That is the product.

### What Her 94% Five-Star Rate Tells You About Who She's Built Her Practice For

A 94% five-star rate across 1,914 reviews means roughly 1,800 sessions that left the client satisfied enough to rate at maximum. At $7.99/min, these are not casual one-time bookings — the financial commitment required to have a meaningful session screens out impulse buyers almost entirely. The clients who book HeeratheIntuitive, reach the session length required to form a real opinion, and then come back are people who found something genuinely useful. That self-selection effect makes her rating more meaningful than a comparable number from a $2.22/min reader where anyone might try once. The 94% is telling you that the clients who are actually right for her model are finding exactly what they came for.

## How to Book Your First Session and Set It Up for the Best Possible Outcome

### What to Bring to a Context-Required Reading — and How Much to Share Upfront

Unlike cold-reading advisors where you withhold information as a test, HeeratheIntuitive explicitly asks for context — and the quality of your session is directly proportional to the quality of the context you bring. Before you call, prepare three things: the core situation in two or three sentences (who is involved, what the central tension is, what has happened most recently), the specific question you want addressed in this session, and any relevant background detail she would need to understand the stakes. Do not overwhelm her with a full history in the opening two minutes — give her the essential frame and let her ask what she needs. The goal is not to tell her everything; it is to give her a working foundation so that her reading is calibrated to your actual situation rather than a generic approximation of it.
