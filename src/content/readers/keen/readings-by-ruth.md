That contradiction is the actual product: you get the unvarnished truth, delivered by someone who waits through your repeated questions.

She currently carries roughly **8,287 ratings at 4.91 stars** and bills **$5.99/min**. The "Intuitive Navigator: 31 Years Unveiling Life's Hidden Truths" tagline pairs three decades of experience with a navigator metaphor - she positions herself as the one who charts your course, not just predicts it. ### Why Lineage Plus Volume Is a Stronger Signal

Any single advisor can claim "born gifted." Far fewer can point to both a family lineage and 41,000 documented readings. The combination is what separates a marketed persona from a proven one. If you are choosing between two 4.9-star readers, the one with lineage and a decade of volume is the safer bet.

Ruth's feedback splits into two consistent themes: direct accuracy, and a patient sweetness that surprises people who expected bluntness.

Also, her directness means she may tell you something you did not want to hear; budget emotionally as well as financially.
