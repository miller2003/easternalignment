## "Inspirational": The Communication Style That Tells You More Than You'd Expect

Every reader in this series carries a communication style tag. Most are Direct, Compassionate, or some combination. Victoria Sands is the only one listed as **Inspirational** — and it's a meaningful distinction.

### What Inspirational Actually Means in a Reading Context

A Direct reader tells you what they see. A Compassionate reader holds you while they do it. An Inspirational reader reframes what they see into a perspective that changes how you relate to it. The primary output isn't information — it's a shift in how you understand your situation.

Her bio captures this explicitly: *"I am more than a psychic. Through my insight and guidance, you will discover that nothing is impossible."* That's not a prediction. That's a promise about what the session will do to your internal state. Her most quoted client feedback reflects it too — one client describes going from feeling "lost my entire life" to feeling "FOUND after having spoken to her." Another says she's "like having a best friend you've never met."

These aren't descriptions of accurate predictions. They're descriptions of perspective transformation. That's what Inspirational style produces at its best: not just answers, but a changed relationship to the questions.

### Who Thrives With This Approach — and Who Finds It Unsatisfying

If you're in a place where a reframe is what you actually need — where you've been stuck in a painful loop of the same thoughts about the same situation and can't get out of it — an Inspirational reader can do something a purely predictive reader can't. She doesn't just tell you what will happen; she helps you see your situation differently enough that what happens next feels like something you can navigate.

If what you want is cold specificity — a precise yes/no with a timeline, a factual read on what someone is thinking, a structured prediction you can verify — her style will likely feel like it redirects you rather than answers you. The Keen AI review summary is careful but clear: *"some express concerns about pricing and prediction timelines."* Clients who came for hard predictions and received perspective shifts coded that as a miss. Those are the 4% who didn't leave five stars.

## No Tarot, No Crystal Ball — But the Profile Lists Four Tools. What's Actually Going On?

Her approach section states it plainly: *"I do not rely on tarot cards, crystal balls, or pendulums."* Yet her tools section lists Chakra Cleansing, Aura Cleansing, Pendulum, and Feng Shui.

Her core work — what happens when you call or chat — is tool-free: direct energy reading, spirit channeling, and what she calls "vivid visual impressions" of you and the person you're asking about. The listed tools represent optional healing and environmental work she can do alongside or after a reading, not instruments she uses to generate the reading itself.

### Her Actual Method: What Happens in a Session

When she connects, she describes receiving information in multiple forms simultaneously: visual impressions, direct spirit speech, and sometimes an impulse to draw or write while listening. She explicitly positions herself not as someone who interprets symbols but as someone who translates directly from Spirit — acting as an instrument rather than an analyst.

This is substantively different from both tarot-based reading and pure clairvoyance. It's closer to full-channel mediumship — the kind of perception where the reader is less processing information than receiving and transmitting it. Her Remote Viewing skill adds a directional layer: the ability to perceive a specific person or location at a distance, independent of what the client shares. Together, these create a reading that works from outside the client's own frame of reference rather than from within it.
