Most people associate Feng Shui with home arrangement and spatial design. In traditional Chinese and Asian metaphysical practice, Feng Shui is considerably more than that — it is the study of how environmental energy flows shape personal circumstances, relationships, and fortune. A reader who can access Feng Shui principles brings a specific question into a session: not just *what is the energy around this person?* but *what is the environmental context shaping this situation, and how is it influencing the trajectory?* For clients dealing with persistent patterns — relationships that keep failing in the same way, career opportunities that consistently fall through, recurring financial blocks — the Feng Shui lens can surface environmental energy dimensions that standard card or clairvoyant readings do not reach.
