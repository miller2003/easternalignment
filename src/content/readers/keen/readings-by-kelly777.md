Remote viewing is listed explicitly in her skill set  -  and it's genuinely unusual on Keen. Most readers offer clairvoyance (seeing impressions and imagery). Remote viewing is a more directed form of perception: the ability to tune into a specific person, location, or situation at a distance without being given information about it first.

### What This Changes About the Kind of Reading She Can Give

For relationship questions specifically, remote viewing is a meaningful capability. Instead of working from what you tell her about someone, she can attempt to perceive that person's current emotional state, intentions, or energy field directly. When it works, the result is information you couldn't have fed her  -  which is the clearest signal of genuine perception rather than reflection.

Client feedback on her accuracy in relationship readings  -  particularly around "what is this person feeling right now" and "what are their true intentions"  -  is one of the most consistent themes across her reviews. That's the kind of question remote viewing is structurally suited to answer.

### The Question Types Where This Has a Real Edge

- What is my person of interest actually feeling about the situation right now?
- Is this person being honest with me about their intentions?
- What's the energy around a specific place, situation, or decision I'm about to make?

These are questions where a reader working purely from what you share can only take you so far. A reader who can independently tune in to the subject of your question has a different starting point.
