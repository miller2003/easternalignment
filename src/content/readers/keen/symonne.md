That's the four-gift stack working as designed: perceive the undercurrent, name it, hand it back as something usable.

## The 3-Star Review That Tells You How to Use Her

We quote the uncomfortable review because it's the most useful one on her page. **User89180232** (Oct 2025): *"She was accurate with connecting to my fears, but my fears are not always truths. She talked about them like they were and I was spiraling pretty hard after the call... I'm giving 3 stars because she read the energy of my fears accurately… but the big picture was not accurate."*

Sit with what that review actually documents: **she read the client's fears flawlessly** — the client admits it — and the friction came from the *weight* of hearing them spoken as fact. That's not an accuracy failure; it's an intensity warning, and it's the single best piece of session-prep advice available for this advisor: SYMONNE reads the emotional undercurrent *directly*, without anesthesia. If you've been avoiding naming a fear to yourself, a session with her will name it in the first five minutes. Clients who are ready for that describe breakthroughs (**Rariel8**: *"she is the real deal!"*). Clients who aren't describe spiraling.

A second minority pattern to know: **User9005001** found her *"defensive"* when challenged — *"telling me to 'hear' her."* Twenty-three years of reading people creates conviction; if you push back mid-read, expect friction rather than retreat. Her regulars read the same trait as integrity: she won't soften a message to win an argument.
