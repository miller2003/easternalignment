## What "Shamanic" Means in Practice

Twenty years of tarot practice — started, per her bio, within her friends, family and community before she ever went commercial — shapes her style: readings as **reflections**, not verdicts. Her goal, in her words: *"Nothing makes me happier than to know I can make your life easier for clarity."*

But her terms also draw lines most readers blur. **One person per reading** — asking about four love interests "confuses yourself, me and the Universe." **No exact timeframes** — freewill makes them fictions. **No baby-gender or pregnancy predictions.** And a request for genuine intentions: no testing her for fun.

This is boundary-setting as a service feature. The boundaries concentrate the reading: one person, one question, full energy. Her soulmate and twin-flame work lives inside that frame — and her clients describe the results with the specific diction of empowerment rather than dependence.
