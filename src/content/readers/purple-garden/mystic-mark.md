## What Scrying Actually Is — and Why It Matters That He Uses Both

Most people are familiar with tarot as a divination method. Scrying is less understood. At its core, scrying is the practice of seeking information through a reflective or translucent medium — classically a crystal ball or a bowl of water, but in practice any surface that the reader can use to focus intuitive attention. Unlike tarot, scrying has no fixed card meanings and no prescribed symbolic vocabulary. It operates through direct intuitive perception, not through a structured symbolic system.

This is the relevant distinction: **tarot and scrying are not two versions of the same thing.** They are methodologically independent. Tarot works symbolically and structurally; scrying works intuitively and impressionistically. A reader who employs both is not doing redundant work — they are running two channels that can corroborate each other, or contradict each other, or reveal aspects of the situation that each method alone would miss.

### Why Triangulation Matters for Complex Situations

The client profile that gets the most from a dual-method reader is one where the situation is genuinely layered. Simple, binary questions — "is this person coming back?" — don't necessarily need two methods. What benefits from triangulation is the kind of situation where the tarot spread shows one thing, but something doesn't add up — where the cards suggest a clear direction but the client's gut says otherwise, or where the external circumstances seem to contradict what the energy of the relationship is actually doing.

Scrying can surface what the structured symbolic read cannot: the underlying current beneath the surface pattern. When the two methods agree, the reading carries a weight that a single-channel session rarely does. When they diverge, the divergence itself is information — and a skilled reader uses it to unpack complexity rather than paper over it.

Mark has been doing this across 13,274 sessions since 2020. That's six years of practicing the dual-method approach on a platform where most readers default to a single tool. The 4.9 rating across that volume is the market's verdict on whether the approach delivers clarity or confusion.
