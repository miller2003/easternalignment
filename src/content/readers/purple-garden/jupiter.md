Love problems don't translate cleanly — the vocabulary of a situationship, a suegra, a *relación complicada* lives in the language the feelings live in.

Júpiter states it plainly in his bio: *"I am from Colombia so Spanish is my native language, in case you speak Spanish too."* And his feed confirms the clientele found him. **Rotsami** (August 22), five stars, in Spanish: *"Muy buena lectura, estoy encantada y agradecida por toda la información que me dió con palabras claras y hasta con tiempos aproximados.
