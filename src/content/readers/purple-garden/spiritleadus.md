## The Protocol — Read It as an Accuracy Feature

Her bio spends more words on intake than most readers spend on their whole profile, and the rules are unambiguous:

- **One topic, one clear question.** *"If you list multiple questions... I may have to take the very first question that I see listed."*
- **Describe your situation.** Background, context, the connection to you, details of the person of interest. *"If there isn't a description... then I will not be able to complete your order at all."*
- **One POI per order.** *"If you come into my queue and give me a list of people to look into with no details, I may have to send the order back."*

She even pre-empts the worry that stops hesitant clients: *"Following the instructions here DOES NOT RUIN YOUR READING. But it allows for your reading to be done in a timely manner... I tap in off your writing."*

That last phrase is the technical key. Her reading style is **writing-triggered** — your description is the tuning fork.
