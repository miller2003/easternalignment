That distinction — fluid dates, steady outcomes — is the heart of this review, and one client's four-day receipt proves it better than we ever could.

## The 4-Day Receipt

**Karen** (August 23): *"I had a reading with Sneha on Aug 18th and she was sure my POI would reach out in a few days and she said she sees him reaching out this week. **She's right! Just 4 days later out of the blue he texted!** Sneha is also really kind and was reassuring me!"*

Read the anatomy of that receipt. A specific event (contact), a specific window (this week), a client skeptical enough to wait — and then the text arrives, *out of the blue*, on day four. That's the kind of prediction that can't hide when it fails, and the client came back to confirm it in writing. It's the cleanest dated receipt in her feed, but not the only flavor of verification. **Diane** (August 14) describes the minimal-info version: *"I felt like she was able to read my thoughts and concerns without me having to share any of that with her. It was over chat, and all I shared were our names, dates of birth, and my question."* Two data points in, a full read out — and it *"resonated."*

## Consistency as the Product

Here's the trait her regulars value most, and it's one most review sites never think to measure: **steadiness across sessions.** **Trin** (August 14): *"Timelines are hard to pinpoint, but Sneha has remained consistent with her prediction. Even though I have been waiting for a while now to see if it comes to pass, **her consistency is what builds trustworthiness to me. It really feels like she is telling me what she sees, not just what I hope to hear**."*

Understand why that matters. The dirty secret of multi-reader platforms is answer-shopping: ask five readers, keep the prediction you like. A reader who shifts her story between sessions feeds that dysfunction; a reader who tells you the same thing in March and in August is giving you something rarer than optimism — a fixed point. Sneha is openly upfront about the mechanism: her service note says time frames are fluid, *"and if that is what you are looking for I am not your reader."* The dates may flex; the read doesn't. That's the deal, and 66% of her feed — **fourteen repeat clients, nikhelen at 17 reviews, Vinh at 11, Jenica at 10** — signed up for it.
