We track a lot of ratios in this job. Likes versus dislikes. Ratings versus session counts. But we have never — across a hundred-plus profiles — published this sentence before: **23 dislikes out of 9,934 total ratings.**

That's Jashodha Starlyte's ledger on Purple Garden: **9,911 thumbs up, 23 thumbs down, a 99.8% positive ratio** across **34,953 readings since 2017**. Her rating sits at a perfect **5.0**, and the platform has stamped its own verdict on top with the badge that matters most — **most_accurate**, which is not a title an advisor gives herself. It's a user vote, computed from the people who paid.

And here's the detail most reviews will miss: she's not just a Purple Garden phenomenon. On **Purple Ocean** — the sister reading app run by the same company — the same advisor carries another **9,758 positive reviews against 23 negative ones**. Two platforms, one reputation, identical numbers. When a record reproduces across two independent audiences, it stops being luck.

## What "Intuitive Energy Guide" Means in Practice

Her bio explains the method, and it's more formal than most: *"I studied tarot from various schools to help with perspectives & knowledge upgrade. Also studied yoga, meditation & various other healing techniques, occult modalities."*

That phrase — **various schools** — is worth pausing on. Tarot has lineages, and readers trained in several of them can switch interpretive frames when a spread demands it: the psychological lens for the "why did he pull away" question, the traditional-symbolism lens for timing, the intuitive overlay when the cards are quiet. Add the meditation and energy-healing background and you get her clients' most repeated observation: readings that are **accurate without being harsh**.

Her own words on the goal: *"I am now truly grateful to help, guide & be of service to others in a way that they receive the truth in a positive & inspiring way."* Truth, delivered kindly. It's the hardest combination in this trade to sustain — and her ratio suggests she has.

## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

Her style is **warm**, and that's the caveat: if your situation needs the truth delivered like a splash of cold water — no cushioning, no gentleness — Jashodha will still tell you the truth, but she'll tell it kindly. Clients who specifically want blunt, no-sugar-coating delivery should book one of Purple Garden's hard-edged readers instead; we've reviewed several. Kindness here is not evasion — her accuracy badge and the prediction-tracking reviews settle that — it's simply her voice.

The second note is availability. A_Kiara's *"are you coming back online?"* is the queue talking. She was offline at our verification snapshot; use the notify-me button rather than camping the app, and expect the wait when a 99.8% ratio is the reason for it.
