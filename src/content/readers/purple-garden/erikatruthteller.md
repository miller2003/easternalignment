## The Shuffle You Can Watch

Her profile states the ritual as policy: *"I shuffle my cards live on camera for authenticity with every reading. If that is something you do not prefer, please choose another reader."* In an industry where "trust me" is the standard verification method, Erika built hers into the session: you watch the deck, the cut, the fall. Clients notice. **Jhanvi**: *"it was so cool how the card fell out in this reading. I will report back as the time line gets closer."* The shuffle is entertainment and evidence in one gesture — and it's the reason her accuracy claims are checkable rather than anecdotal.

## The Step-by-Step Doctrine

Her prediction philosophy is the most quotable in this batch: *"I predict in a step-by-step way. For example: if you're single, asking when you will be married is not the next step — the next step would be meeting someone or a relationship. The next step to your goal is the focus I will predict."*

Read that as epistemology, not hedging. Milestone predictions are falsifiable (either the step happens or it doesn't), which is why her feed fills with verification reports: **gillybean**: *"Had a reading earlier in the month about communication from a specific person. Prediction of contact was related to the number 12. **Received a text on the 12th** of the month. Spot on accuracy."* **Rashmi12345**: *"Will be back once it comes to pass. Your last reading was on point."* **CLS**: *"Everything feels in line with where I currently am, and I can see how it will progress the way Erika has predicted."*

The doctrine also explains her boundaries, stated openly: she doesn't see exact dates, locations, names, or appearances; no medical or legal questions; *"future is not set in stone — energy is always changing."* A reader who predicts only what she claims to see — and lets you watch the cards — is operating with unusual integrity for a premium rate.

## The Premium Math — and How to Spend It

**Chat runs $14.99/min; voice $24.99/min.** That's top-tier pricing: a 15-minute voice session runs about **$375**. The **$30 new-client credit** covers the first two minutes — a taste, not a session.

## The Honest Small Print

Three notes. **First, the price is the filter.** A minority of clients have called the cost hard to justify for a reading that didn't resonate — at this rate, use the live-shuffle session to verify fit before building a habit. **Second, the format rules.** One question at a time on chat; video requests carry strict question limits and no new questions mid-order. She runs a tight ship because her queue is deep — the trimming protects the reading quality you're paying for. **Third, the no-endpoints rule.** If you need "will we marry by June," her step-wise doctrine will reroute you to "the next step toward it." Clients wanting hard dates should book a timeline reader; clients tired of broken dates should book her.
