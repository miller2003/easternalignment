Ayla is the kind of advisor you call when you're not shopping for a prophecy — you're shopping for the truth. On Purple Garden she goes by "Ayla Love Resolution," and after nearly 15,000 readings since 2021 at a steady 4.8 stars, she has earned the platform's Top Rated tag without ever promising to fix your love life for you. That tension is the single most useful thing to understand before you book her: the quality that makes her less satisfying to some clients is the exact quality that makes her trustworthy.

## She doesn't pick one lane — she triangulates

Most readers hang their pitch on a single tool. Ayla's profile lists clairvoyance, tarot, numerology, and energy insight — four different lenses she blends depending on the question. That's rarer than it sounds, and it matters for relationship work specifically, because "is this going anywhere" is almost never a one-dimensional question. When you're tangled up in a dynamic rather than asking for a single fact, a reader who can cross-check an impression against a spread and a number tends to give you something sturdier than a hunch.

## The catch — and why it's actually the selling point

Here's the part I genuinely like about her, framed as a warning: Ayla will not hand you a clean yes-or-no and send you on your way. If what you want is a two-minute "he loves you / he doesn't" hit, there are faster, cheaper readers on the same platform. Ayla's reads run longer and dig — she maps the pattern, names the dynamic, and points at where the work is. Clients who want to be comforted sometimes find that annoying. Clients who want to actually understand their love life tend to come back. A 4.8 across nearly 15,000 readings is, in no small part, a record of people returning because she told them something true rather than something nice.

## What a session actually feels like

Purple Garden lets you read by live video, voice, or chat, and for Ayla the video option is the unlock. You can watch her pull cards and read energy in real time — which does half the trust-building on a platform that, frankly, makes no promise that any single reading will be "accurate." The $2.99/min rate puts her in Purple Garden's budget tier, and that matters more than it looks: it lets you book a real session instead of a nervous three-minute test. New clients get $30 in credit on a first purchase, which covers roughly ten minutes — about how long a first Ayla read needs to get past the pleasantries and into the actual question.

## One honesty note about the platform

Purple Garden now backs your first session with a given advisor with a satisfaction guarantee (up to $50 in credit), but it does not warrant that a reading will land. The smart play is to open the green/red review split on her profile before you connect: you see exactly how many clients left a happy face versus a frustrated one, and Ayla's green side is decisively the heavy one. Treat the rating as a signal about consistency, not a promise about your specific outcome, and you'll use her well.

## Is Ayla right for you?

**Book her if** you want a relationship read that respects your intelligence, you're comfortable with impression-led insight over a rigid card spread, and you'd rather pay $2.99/min for substance than $20/min for theater.

**Skip her if** you need a timed prediction ("he'll call Tuesday"), you want career or finance as the main course, or you're not willing to do any of the emotional work she'll point toward. She's a love-resolution reader, not a life-coach-for-everything.

**Start with the $30 credit.** It's enough for a proper first session with Ayla — ask one real relationship question, watch how she works, and decide from there whether the multi-tool style is yours.
