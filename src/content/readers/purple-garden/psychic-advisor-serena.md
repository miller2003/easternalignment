Serena is the rare Purple Garden advisor whose reputation precedes the platform. She's an internationally known psychic medium who's been reading since 2005, works without tools (claircognizance and clairvoyance — a "clear knowingness" rather than a deck), and reads auras (her aura work has even been featured in Cosmopolitan). On Purple Garden she holds a 4.9. The thing to understand about her is simple: she tells it like she sees it, and she's proud of that.

## Two decades, no deck

Twenty years of mediumship is a different weight class from the platform's newer names. Serena connects to your energy and aura directly, taps past loved ones for messages, and reads love, career, and money without laying a single card. That tools-free approach is either exactly what you want or exactly what you avoid — and she's clear about which clients she's right for. The longevity plus the name recognition means you're not betting on an unknown; you're booking a reader with a track record that exists outside any one app.

## The catch — and why it's the reason to trust her

Serena is blunt, and she says so herself: "I do not sugar coat or offer fairy tales. I tell it like I see it." For a client having a fragile night, that can land hard. But read the rest of her profile and the pattern is the opposite of careless — she's compassionate and non-judgemental, LGBTQIA+ friendly, and she frames hard truths as guidance from your guides trying to protect you. A reader who warns you up front that you might not like the answer is a reader who isn't shaping the answer to keep you. On a platform with no blanket accuracy guarantee, that kind of intellectual honesty is the closest thing to a quality signal you'll get.

## What a session actually feels like

Two practical notes. First, she's on Purple Garden part-time alongside a full-time career, so when she's not live, hit the bell alert and she'll hop on when she can — patience pays here. Second, she doesn't do free follow-up questions in chat and she won't read for anyone who left a negative review; those are boundaries, not attitude, and they keep her reads clean. At $4.99/min she's mid-tier, and the $30 new-client credit covers about six minutes — lead with your sharpest question. Purple Garden's video option lets you watch her connect and read your aura live, which is the right way to experience a no-tools medium.

## Is Serena right for you?

**Book her if** you want an internationally known, tools-free medium who gives clear, honest answers and you're ready to hear them.

**Skip her if** you need a reader who validates a specific outcome, you want comfort over clarity, or you expect free back-and-forth follow-ups.

**Start with the $30 credit.** Set the bell alert, ask one real question, and see whether two decades of straight-talk mediumship is your kind of truth.
