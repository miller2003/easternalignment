Purple Garden's pricing has a rule everyone assumes: video costs more. Ophelia's profile breaks the rule — **video at $2.99/min, cheaper than her $4.49 chat** — and in doing so offers the best-kept secret on the platform: a face-to-face session with a recommended-badge reader for less than typing costs anywhere else.

The practice behind the price: **"psychic therapy"** — her own term, in her own service title — built on *"the power of tarot, crystals, dreams, astrology, intuition, and more."* She's been offering it since **2015**, a decade of practice, with **28,251 platform readings since 2021** at a **4.8** and the **recommended badge**. The platform itself flags her as booking up quickly — the queue signal that matters more than any marketing copy.

## What "Psychic Therapy" Means as a Practice

The term is precise. Therapy sessions aim at **progress** — and Ophelia's feed shows clients measuring that progress. **Alan1456**: *"More forward progress has focused but it's still baby steps."* That's a client describing a multi-session arc, tracked in steps, not a one-off prophecy. Her tools give the session its structure; the therapy frame gives it a direction.

And the honesty contract is the practice's spine, stated by a client in the plainest possible terms: *"she is quick and honest can say she will not give you false hopes."* In a market where false hope is the cheapest product on the shelf, a reader whose regulars cite her refusal to sell it is doing the opposite of the worst stereotype.

## The Price, Translated Into What You're Actually Buying

**Video is $2.99/min; chat and voice $4.49.** The inversion changes the calculus: **book video.** You get face-to-face work — cards visible, expressions visible — at the platform's lowest camera rate. Purple Garden's **$30 free credit on your first purchase** covers **ten free video minutes** — a full therapy session, essentially prepaid, face to face.

For clients who've found chat readings impersonal and voice readings unverifiable, this is the rare profile where the most accountable format is also the cheapest.

## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

The caveat is the **honesty contract**: "no false hopes" means the read you get is the read there is. If tonight's version of you needs the hopeful version regardless of the cards, Ophelia is the wrong instrument tonight — her regulars cite her refusal as her virtue. Come ready for truth delivered with therapeutic patience.

Second: the 4.7-4.8 band, honestly stated, sits below the platform's perfect scores. The badge, the decade of practice, and the multi-year client logs are the better instruments for judging what you'll get.

## Should You Book Ophelia?

**Book her if** you want the therapy-framed reading — structured, honest, progress-oriented — from a recommended-badge reader with a decade of practice, and you want it face-to-face at the platform's cheapest video rate with ten free minutes from the credit.

**Skip her if** you want hopeful framing over honest framing, or the absolute cheapest chat-only minutes. Both exist; her lane is the honest middle.

Either way, the credit does the entry work. Bring the situation, book the video, and let the session work. If Deannak's multi-year log is the pattern, "spot on" will be a sentence you write again next year.
