**Book Andrew Angel if** you have one specific question — about love, a decision, a person — and you want it answered directly by a fourth-generation psychic with 55,003 readings and a perfect 5.0. **Skip him if** you want an open-ended "general reading" or a session about health, pregnancy, or someone who has passed: he declines all of these, in writing, at the top of his own profile. That refusal is the first thing to understand about Andrew Angel — and, we'd argue, a large part of why the 5.0 exists.

## The Velvet Rope

Most advisor profiles are funnels, engineered to convert every visitor into paid minutes. Andrew Angel's opens with a door policy:

*"For the most detailed reading please ask Specific questions you have (Don't do General Reading's)... I don't do health/pregnancy/related questions, I don't pick up on people who has passed on or talk about other advisors' predictions... one topic per order."*

Read that the way you'd read a restaurant with a dress code and a waitlist. A reader who accepts *everything* — vague questions, unlimited scope, any topic — is optimizing for minutes sold. A reader who narrows the brief is optimizing for what happens *inside* the minutes: one question, full focus, zero dilution. His instructions amount to a service-level agreement between him and your wallet: *"I expect that my clients want accuracy, clear simplicity, honesty, and efficiency, and these are my guiding principles for every call."*

Velvet ropes exist for two reasons: to keep the room good, and because the room is good. Fifty-five thousand readings, a platform "highest rated" badge, and a perfect 5.0 later, both apply here.

## Fourth Generation, Sixteen Years — and a Palm-Reading Bonus

Under the door policy sits a pedigree stated with almost suspicious modesty: *"I have over 16 Years of experience and have done psychic reading over the phone, chat, video and in person as well. I am a fourth generation psychic."*

His method, in his own words: gathering information by *"seeing, hearing and feeling"* the people and situations around your question — and, unusually in our reading of hundreds of bios, he claims to connect not only with his own spiritual guides but with *his clients'* guides as well. Whether you read that as theology or technique, the practical implication is the same: he's working both ends of the connection.

His listed specialties run from psychic and tarot readings to love readings and **dream analysis** — a genuinely underserviced niche — and he's recently added **palm readings via video** (send a video of your palm alongside your questions). Almost no one else on the platform offers a second divination modality inside the same booking. One honest note on his worldview, because it tells you what kind of reading you'll get: he works from thoughts, feelings, and actions — *"You can often change the future by understanding YOUR THOUGHTS, FEELINGS, ACTIONS AND THOSE OF THE PEOPLE AROUND YOU"* — which means his sessions aim at *your* leverage, not just your fate. That's a feature: a reading you can act on beats a prophecy you can only await.

## What 23,069 Likes Sound Like

We pulled Andrew's 150 most recent client reviews and ran two analyses. The first is language: **"accurate" appears eleven times, "kind" ten, "honest" eight, "detail" six, "quick" five.** His marketing says "accuracy, clear simplicity, honesty, efficiency"; his clients' vocabulary matches it word for word. Alignment between the promise and the feed is rarer than you'd think.

The second analysis is the loyalty structure — and here's where Andrew differs from every other advisor we've audited this month. Some readers' feeds are dominated by a few superfans (one beloved Kasamba reader has a single client holding 31 reviews). Andrew's is the opposite: **thirteen different repeat reviewers, none holding more than six reviews.** No fan club. Just a broad, evenly distributed base of people who tried him, got what they came for, and came back a sane number of times. That's not charisma retention. That's *consistency* retention — the kind produced by a process, which is exactly what his rules are.

The reviews themselves carry the specifics. **A2Jules** (August 6): *"What Andrew was able to show through spirit guidance was spot on and came to fruition, just as he said it would, with key details following through... after using several advisors, he is by far the best."* Note the witness quality — a client who has *used several advisors* and names a winner, citing key details that later checked out. **Robert** (August 8) confirms the mechanics: *"he was direct and on point with reading. quick to respond as well."* **SuzieLeiasina36** (August 12): *"He answered my question directly and explained the cards beautifully."* Direct answers, explained reasoning, fast delivery — the SLA, performed nightly.

## The Slot Problem — and the Two Other Harmless Quirks

Now the friction, and you'll notice it all flatters him. First: **getting Andrew is genuinely hard.** **Amelia13** (August 24) says it best: *"He is my God sent... Only if u are lucky, u wil get his slot."* When regulars treat a reader's calendar as a lottery, that's demand doing the talking. The practical fix: catch him off-peak, or set a notification and take the slot when it opens rather than waiting for a "good time."

Second: **come prepared.** One question, specific, written down before you connect. This is technically a rule, but it's really a gift — clients who arrive focused report the fastest, densest readings on the platform, and your $30 credit stretches furthest when no minute is spent circling.

Third, and we mention it only because it made us smile: **his profile has the occasional typo** ("getz answer"). A working fourth-generation psychic, not a copywriter. The polish went into the readings; the 23,069 likes confirm the allocation.
