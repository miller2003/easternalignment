**Book Enlighten Matt if** your question is about love — his feelings, her intentions, the marriage gone quiet, the one who got away — and you want a straight-shooting male empath who meditates into your energy, tells you what's actually there, and keeps talking afterward. **Skip him if** your question is career, or if you need a reader who'll make your decision for you; he declines both by written policy.

## The Love-Only Specialist With the Male Decode

Matt's lane is the narrowest and clearest in this batch: **"Love & Relationship Only. No Career/Job Questions."** Within it, he covers the full map — the new person's feelings, the long partner who's gone unreadable, suspected infidelity, the marriage struggling to reconnect, the person you can't stop thinking about — and he offers one niche almost no other profile names: **the gender bridge**. *"Maybe you don't need a reading, just need a male perspective on things, ladies. And guys, maybe you have a hard time understanding women. Allow me to give you my best insight."*

The file behind the niche: **25,948 readings since 2018** at **99.3% positive — 8,664 positive against 63 negative**, with a **4.9-star average**. Seven years, near-perfect approval, one lane. His regulars don't shop around anymore; they renew.

## The Method: Meditation, Not Props

Matt's instrument is described with unusual transparency: *"I sit in meditation while connecting with your energy so I can get the answers I need — whether it's good or bad. No sugar coating here."* And the empathy mechanism he claims is the deep version: *"When you send me a video and I tap into your energy, I can feel whatever it is that's on your mind or feeling. So it's like I'm experiencing it all for myself. So when I tell you I feel your pain — trust me, I really do. And the excitement and happiness too."*

The intake supports precision: **a 30-second video with your name, date of birth, and brief context** — plus the name and DOB (ideally a photo) of anyone else in the read. He's explicit about why: *"I ask this so I can assure you the most precise accuracy and to give you your money's worth."*

The feed validates the whole stack. **Deb**, his most-quoted regular: *"Matt is very on point — **never tells you what you want to hear, only tells you what you need to hear**, what he sees, either good or bad."* **barbie**: *"Without telling him, he brings up things that confirm a lot of stuff. My go-to when I need clarity."* **henyf3588** logs a cold pickup for the skeptics: *"I don't even let you know what the problem is, but you know about leaving the country... Matt right away picked up the question. 5 stars."* **cindyjs**: *"He picks up on energy quickly and he's very articulate on conveying what you need to know."*

## The Two-Question Protocol — and the Ethics Around It

Matt's structure is the tightest in this review: **two questions per session**, answered fully, with a principled boundary attached — *"please don't ask me to make your decisions for you. I can only give you the information I saw in my meditation and make a guided recommendation."*

That's the ethical spine of his practice: information in, agency preserved. Combined with the video intake, it makes his sessions unusually efficient — no twenty-minute backstory, because the video did that work free. What's left is the answer, articulated. **cindyjs**' word for it — "articulate" — is the right one; his reviews repeatedly praise how clearly he conveys what he sees.

Then there's the aftercare. *"I take my time with answering any questions they may have after the reading."* In a niche where post-session silence is the standard complaint, Matt's follow-through is a competitive advantage — and a reason his client list behaves like a friendship circle: *"I'm one of the more laid-back psychics and tend to make clients my friends more than anything, but on a professional level."*

## The $7.99 Math

**Chat and voice run the same $7.99/min** — no format tax. The **$30 new-client credit covers nearly four minutes**; a focused session with both questions runs about **$64 for eight minutes** or **$120 for fifteen**. One reviewer's remark — *"I just don't have the $79 to finish my conversation with him"* — reads as complaint and compliment at once: his sessions are worth finishing, and they're honest enough that people want to. Budget accordingly: write your two questions before you connect, send the video, and let the credit absorb the opening.

His disclaimer is standard-issue honesty: *"Time frames can change due to free will and energy."* His regulars agree — **gen**: *"Time frames are difficult to predict, but overall his input and insight has been spot on."* Book him for what's true now and what's coming; treat the calendar as a sketch.

## The Honest Small Print

Three notes. **First, the two-question limit.** Pick your two before ordering; a third question waits for the next session. **Second, the scope.** Career, education, legal, pregnancy — declined. Love and life-change guidance is the whole menu. **Third, the no-sugarcoat register.** "DON'T PICK ME IF YOU PREFER SUGAR COATED READINGS" is literally in his header — he'd rather lose the booking than soften the read. If you've read this far, that's probably the feature.

**The she-won't-tell-me client.** A partner or POI gone unreadable — his male-side decode of male (and female) motivations is the specialty's core.

**The friendship-tier regular.** If your reading life works best with a steady advisor who remembers you, Matt's laid-back retainer model — and post-reading patience — is built for it.
