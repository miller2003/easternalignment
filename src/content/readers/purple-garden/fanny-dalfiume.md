Fanny Dalfiume is the reader you book when you want to experiment without risk. Nearly 19,000 readings since 2021 at a 4.6-star rating, starting at $1.99/min — she is Purple Garden's high-volume, budget-tier option for anyone who likes a metaphysical frame wrapped around real tarot work. The rating looks modest next to the platform's army of 5.0s, and that's exactly the point: at this price and this volume, a 4.6 is honesty you can use.

## Metaphysics meets tarot

Fanny's profile describes her offering divination "using different methods," blending metaphysics with tarot. That hybrid is the draw for clients who want more than a straight card spread — the metaphysics layer lets a session move between symbolic card work and broader spiritual interpretation, which suits open-ended life and relationship questions with a mystical frame. If you like your guidance served with a sense of soul-design rather than cold prediction, her toolkit fits.

On Purple Garden she reads by live video, voice, and chat. The $1.99 start is her chat rate, and at that price the barrier to a first session is close to zero — which is the whole reason to try her. If you want the most from the metaphysics layer, open a live video call and watch the cards turn; if you just want a quick, low-stakes read on a shifting situation, chat is the cheapest way to stay in touch with a reader you're still evaluating.

## Why 4.6 is a feature, not a flaw

Here's the uncomfortable truth the 5.0 crowd hides: a perfect score at low volume is easy to manufacture, and a flawless average on a smaller book often means lighter scrutiny. Fanny carries a 4.6 across nearly 19,000 readings — a stable, real average built on tens of thousands of client encounters. That number is a signal of consistency, not failure. It says "very good, not flawless," and at $1.99/min, very good is extraordinary value.

The honest rating also tells you something about the reader: she isn't buying five-star reviews or cherry-picking clients. The occasional mismatch her reviews show is the natural cost of high volume plus a mystical style some literal-minded clients don't connect with. Read Purple Garden's green/red split on her profile and you'll see the happy side is decisively the heavy one — the frustrated minority are mostly people who wanted a different kind of reader, not a failed one.

That split is the tool to use before you book anyone, and it's especially useful here. A 4.6 average built on nearly 19,000 readings is a far more honest signal than a 5.0 built on a few hundred, because the sample is large enough that no one can quietly bury the misses. Open the green/red breakdown, glance at the recent reviews, and you'll see the shape of her work in minutes — then decide whether the mystical frame is one you want to pay $1.99/min to explore.

## The catch — and why it's the sell

Fanny's metaphysics framing won't suit everyone. Clients who want strict, literal predictions can find the spiritual layer too soft, and her 2021 start makes her newer than the decade veterans. Both are limitations, and both are reasons budget-minded explorers should start with her: at $1.99/min the downside of a mismatch is a few credits, not a premium bill. The low price turns her "not for everyone" rating into a feature — you get to find out cheaply whether her metaphysics-and-tarot voice is yours.

Purple Garden's design supports the experiment: new clients get $30 in credit on a first purchase, and the first session is backed by a satisfaction guarantee up to $50 in credit. There's no blanket promise that any single reading lands, so the platform's own safeguard is to start small and read the reviews.

## Booking Fanny on a budget

At $1.99/min, the $30 new-client credit covers roughly fifteen minutes — a full first session, not a tester. Saved transcripts let you revisit the read afterward, and you can message Fanny up to three times a day following a session if a clarifier hits you later. For regular, low-cost check-ins, few readers on the platform are this affordable at this volume.

Set a timer if you tend to linger — the price makes it easy to keep talking — but for most open-ended questions, fifteen minutes with Fanny is a real reading you can afford to repeat.

## Is Fanny right for you?

**Book her if** you're budget-conscious, you like a metaphysics-and-tarot frame, and you want high volume at a low price with room to experiment. If "very good, not flawless" sounds like an honest deal, she's strong value.

**Skip her if** you need a perfect-5.0 elite reader, or a purely literal, prediction-first session where the mystical layer would frustrate you. A pure-tarot or direct-psychic reader will be a cleaner fit.

**Start with the $30 credit.** Fifteen minutes is enough to ask one real question and discover whether Fanny's blend of metaphysics and tarot resonates — at a price that makes the answer risk-free.
