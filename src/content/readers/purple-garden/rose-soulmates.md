Her credentials are lineage-deep: *"I'm a Psychic Energy reader and soulmate master. ## How the Reading Works — and Why the Rules Exist

Her intake is engineered for accuracy at speed. You give a **first name and date of birth** for the person in question, keep your message short, and she reads the connection from there — *"1–2 clarification questions from the reading itself"* are permitted, which keeps the session centered on what she's actually picking up rather than on client narrative. Skeptics should note what that protocol implies: she's working from the minimum viable data, not from a backstory she could cold-read.

The scope limits are equally deliberate. **No work or career questions. No financial, legal, health, or mental-health readings. No timelines** ("things can always change"). No multi-person readings, no general life forecasts. If that sounds restrictive, it's the other side of precision: every session is one connection, examined deeply. **LadyWarrior**'s review describes the craft under time pressure: *"Really appreciate the rush reading and your time in meditation. Very helpful insight with detailed explanations."*

## The Honest Small Print

Three notes. **First, the no-timeline policy is absolute.** She'll read trajectory, readiness, and movement — *"time will tell but I'm hopeful,"* as one September reviewer put it — but she will not promise March 14th. If you need dates, book a timeline reader; if you're tired of dates that never arrive, her approach will feel like relief. **Second, the lane is narrow.** Career anxiety, legal questions, "read my whole life" — declined. Come with the connection, not the résumé. **Third, the intake brevity.** Long paragraphs in the request box are explicitly discouraged. A client who wrote one reviewer-grade saga before a session might feel constrained; the 99.2% who follow the format don't.
