## The Requirements List: Gimmick, or the Secret to the 5.0?

Her tagline isn't a promise — it's an instruction: **"PLS READ REQUIREMENTS."** The full protocol, published on her profile:

- **First names ONLY** — for you and every party you ask about
- **ONLY 2 questions per reading** — *"Failing to do so will result in a more general reading"*
- **Willingly answer 1 clarification question** during the session
- **No lotto, health, pregnancy, spells, or legal matters** (platform rules)
- **Timeframes:** *"they do not always come through; they are an estimate when they do"*

Most advisors' profiles are written to maximize bookings. This one is written to maximize *accuracy* — and it's willing to lose bookings to do it. That's the tell. Each rule maps to a known failure mode of bad readings: full names invite cold-reading from details; ten questions guarantees ten shallow answers; a clarification exchange lets her verify the connection before charging you for conclusions built on a bad one. And the timeframe disclaimer is the single most honest sentence in the genre — she tells you, before you pay, that dates are estimates when they come at all.

The result is visible in the structure of her feedback: **25,970 positive reviews against 127 negative — 0.5%** — and a **Top Accuracy badge** on a platform where badges are earned from client ratings, not bought. Discipline is the product. The 5.0 is the receipt.
