## The License on the Wall

Scroll her profile and you hit a line almost no Purple Garden bio carries: **"Licensed Psychic of Davenport, IA."** Municipal psychic licensing is rare in the United States — most cities regulate fortune-telling with a basic permit, and almost nobody advertises it. Katherine prints it. Add 15+ years of professional practice and the picture is consistent: this is a registered trade for her, not a username with a vibration.

The volume backs it. **25,000+ readings on Purple Ocean alone** (her own count, and the sister platform's public feed corroborates the scale), plus **27,077 on Purple Garden since 2017** at **98.7% positive — 8,635 positive against 115 negative**. Fifty thousand-plus sessions across two platforms is a body of work; the 4.9-star average across both is the grade.

## The Truth Warning — Read It Before You Book

Her profile opens with the least marketable, most trustworthy paragraph in this review series: *"If you are sensitive to the truth and do not want to hear answers that may not be the best news to your situation, please do not connect with me. I am here to provide what spirit shows for you, whether that is good or bad. Be prepared to hear the truth."*

That paragraph does two jobs. It filters clients — a reader who warns you is a reader who won't flinch later. And it sets the delivery contract: her job is transmission, not consolation. The upside shows in her feed. **Cole**: *"I talked to her in July and everything happened as she said — and sooner. I just talked to her again, will update in time."* **Morgan Rayca**: *"she seemed really spot on with my POI and what he is going through."* **Tamick**: *"I am in the middle of something coming to pass that was mentioned."* Accuracy is the recurring noun; the directness is the price of admission, disclosed upfront.

## The Ten-Lane Menu — Pick Your Lane

Katherine's practice is unusually broad: **love and relationship readings, business and finance outlook, dream interpretation, mediumship, chakra and aura readings, family matters, tarot, paranormal, chakra balancing** — plus **live meditation sessions** available on request. She also frames her philosophy in counselor's language: *"Many have the knowledge deep within them and simply need the right spiritual guidance to find the answers they have buried inside."*

The breadth is a genuine asset — one trusted reader for the love question *and* the money question *and* the dream that won't resolve. It's also the session's main failure mode: order a vague "just read me" and her energy splits across ten lanes. The fix is one word of discipline — **name the lane** ("love, his intentions, next 3 months") — and the same breadth becomes precision.
