Satie Readings has been reading professionally for over 40 years, and on Purple Garden since 2020 she has completed nearly 18,000 readings at a 4.9, with an accuracy rating in the high 80s. She connects with your guides through tarot and reads every session live on camera. The line that defines her, in her own words: "I am NOT a Fortune Teller." For clients who want a mom-vibes reader who still tells the hard truth, she is one of the steadier bets on the platform.

## Forty years in, and she still reads live on camera

Most high-volume readers hide behind text. Satie does the opposite — every reading is conducted live on camera, so you watch the cards pull and the read land in real time. That transparency matters on a platform that makes no promise any single reading will be accurate; you are seeing the work, not taking it on faith. Combined with 40 years of practice, it produces the calm, patterned insight of someone who has seen every relationship shape a client can bring. The 4.9 across nearly 18,000 readings is what that experience looks like at scale, and it is why her regulars describe a "mom vibe" that is also sharply direct.

## Her hard rules (and why they make the reading safer)

Satie is known on her profile for what she will not do: no yes/no questions ("will he call me"), no specific timeframes, no health, legal, or financial reads, and — pointedly — she will not read again for clients who left a negative review. A first-timer can read that as austere, even prickly. But flip it: those boundaries are what keep the session deep instead of theatrical. By refusing to perform a fake prediction ("he will call Tuesday") she protects you from false hope, and by not re-reading unhappy clients she keeps her energy clean for the people she can actually help. The readers who will tell you anything to keep you booking are the ones to fear. Satie's rules are the reason her honest-but-compassionate reputation holds.

## Tarot, guides, and a coach's toolkit

Her method is tarot plus guide connection, but the toolkit is wider than cards: she is trained in relationship coaching, NLP, Law of Attraction, crystal therapy, and is a certified Reiki Master Teacher. That shows up as readings that go past "what is happening" into "what is blocked and how to shift it" — emotional healing, closure, third-party dynamics, reconciliation questions. She specializes in love and relationship, breakups, and "what are they feeling," and she is clear about her limits, which is its own form of trust. Purple Garden's green/red review split on her profile shows the happy faces dominating; read the split before you book so you know what you are walking into.

**Book her if** you want an experienced, camera-transparent love-tarot read, you value blunt compassion over comfort, and you are working a relationship or closure question.
