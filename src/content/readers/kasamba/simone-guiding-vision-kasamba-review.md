## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

Simone's style is **comfort-led**. Her feed's adjectives are calming, not jolting — the "happiness back in your life" mission shapes sessions that soothe before they sharpen. If your situation needs the cold verdict first — the hard-no school — her manner will feel gentle where you wanted steel. That's a fit question, not a flaw; her 99% ledger says the fit matches a great many people.

Second: she's a **generalist** — love, career, dreams, fortune, all competent, none theatrical. If you want a niche specialist (a pure medium, a timing astrologer), compare profiles; her strength is the whole-life steady read.
