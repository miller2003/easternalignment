There's a review on Immense Spark's Kasamba page, from a client named **Dex**, dated this month:

*"I've been coming to Immense for over a decade!"*

Read that twice. Not "I tried her once." Not "great reading." **Over a decade.** On a platform where most client relationships last a session or two, Dex has been returning to the same reader for *ten years* — and came back this month to say, *"As always, she is very good and precise."*

That's the review that made us write this. You can fake a five-star rating. You can curate a testimonial. You cannot fake a decade. So the question this review answers is simple: what kind of reader earns that kind of loyalty — and is she the kind you're looking for?

## 66,152 Readings and Inherited Gifts

The numbers first. Immense Spark has done **66,152 readings on Kasamba since 2007**, holding a 4.8 across nearly two decades of un-erasable reviews. Kasamba doesn't let advisors prune their history, so a 4.8 at that scale is a fact, not a claim.

Her pedigree is the part that explains the loyalty. She writes that she *"inherited psychic abilities from older generations"* — this is a reader who frames her gift as a lineage, not a skill she picked up. She claims 15 years of experience *"helping celebrities and individuals"* (we can't verify the famous names; we can verify the everyday ones, because they keep writing reviews). Her specialties run wide: psychic readings, dream analysis, love, mediumship, and astrology. One reader, most of the bases covered.

The combination that matters: **inherited gift + 15+ years + 66,000 readings + a decade-long client.** That's a reader people *stay* with.

## The Pricing — Premium, With One Rule

Immense Spark sits in premium territory. Listed rates: **$21.99/min chat, $39.99/min voice.** New-client promo: **chat drops to $10.99/min, voice to $19.99/min, plus 3 free minutes.**

At promo rates, a focused 15-minute chat runs about **$132** after the free three. That's upper-mid-range — more than Cosmic Fusion's $48, less than Wisdom and Love's $168. The honest framing: you're paying premium-tier money for a reader whose standout credential is *retention*. Dex didn't come back for a decade because she was cheap. He came back because she was worth it.

**The rule: book chat.** Voice at $19.99/min promo is nearly double chat at $10.99 — and unlike chat, there's no evidence the voice format delivers twice the value. Same reader, same gifts. Use text unless you specifically need to hear a voice.

One transparency note: Kasamba rotates promos, and we've watched card prices shift between visits. Look at what the card shows *your* account before you start. The 3 free minutes are constant.

## The Caveat — and It's the Price, Again

If there's a reason not to book Immense Spark, it's the obvious one. **She is not a budget read.** If you're browsing Kasamba for a cheap curiosity check, there are 5.0 readers on this site at $3.99-$4.99/min who'll serve you better for less. Book Immense Spark when you're looking for the *relationship* Dex has — a reader you can return to across years, who already knows your story when you come back.

The other honest flag: her 4.8 isn't a perfect 5.0. Across 66,000 readings we'd argue a 4.8 you can audit is worth more than a 5.0 you can't, but if you're shopping strictly for the ceiling, she's not it.

## Should You Book Immense Spark?

**Book her if** you're looking for a reader to stay with — not a one-off, but a guide you can return to across months and years, the way Dex has for a decade. The promo makes the first session affordable enough to test that fit.

**Skip her if** you want the cheapest possible minutes, or you need a perfect-5.0 specialist in a single narrow method. She's a premium generalist built for retention, not a bargain built for volume.

The first 3 minutes are free. Ask her the question you keep re-asking. If you find yourself, like Dex, coming back a decade later saying *"as always, she is very good and precise"* — well, that's the entire point of a reader like this.
