Here's where the honest review earns its keep. At **$24.49/min chat**, an eight-minute call costs about **$196** — and if the session yields three insights, that's $65 per insight. Her five-star clients accept that arithmetic because their sessions run as ongoing counsel: the reunion campaign, the abuse-recovery year, the marriage triage. The arithmetic punishes single-session outcome-shoppers, and the star column collects the difference.

If you book her, book her deliberately: **one question, stated in the first message; a defined session length; and the intention to return** rather than resolve everything at once. Voice at $39.99/min is for the sessions where her empathic presence matters more than the transcript.
