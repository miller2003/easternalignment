> *"The theory is that soul mates come from past lives. Soul mate connection is something that is not under our control... it is something that is like natural and something that can be felt from inside."*

That's not a tagline — it's an operating system. If you're on Kasamba at 1 a.m. asking whether the person you can't stop thinking about is *actually* yours, this is a reader who will not treat the question as silly. She's built her entire practice — **35,548 readings since 2017** — around treating it as the most serious question there is.

The numbers first, as always: nine years on the platform, roughly **twelve sessions a day**, a **95% positive ratio** (7,563 likes against 368 dislikes), and pricing that starts at **$2.99/min on chat**. ## Why a Channeler Reads Differently

Most love readers on Kasamba work through tools — tarot, astrology, numerology. Divine Princess works through **connection**. Channeling means she's not interpreting a spread; she's reporting what comes through when she links to your energy and the person you're asking about.

The practical difference shows up in what her clients emphasize. Her sessions aren't famous for card-by-card drama; they're famous for *recognition* — the "how did she know that" moments — and for pairing the spiritual read with what her bio calls *"not only spiritual but practical advises as well."* You leave knowing what the energy says **and** what to do about it this week.

Her range is wider than the soulmate thesis suggests, too. Kasamba lists her across five categories: **Psychic Mediums, Love Psychics, Dream Analysis, Career Forecasts, and general Psychic Readings**. The dream analysis pairing is worth noting — if the person you're asking about keeps showing up in your dreams, that's literally her intersection: dreams, energy, and union.

## The Price, Translated Into What You're Actually Buying

Divine Princess charges **$2.99/min chat and $4.99/min voice** — one of the wider chat/voice gaps we've seen, and it shapes the advice: **your first session should be a chat session.** Same reader, same channel, 40% cheaper per minute.

Apply Kasamba's new-client promo — **3 free minutes + 50% off** — and a focused 15-minute chat works out to about **$18**: three minutes free, twelve at roughly $1.50. For that you get a licensed channeler with nine years and thirty-five thousand readings of reps.

Here's the reframe that matters at the budget tier: a low rate only counts as "affordable" if the session *resolves* something. A $2.99 reader who talks in circles for twenty minutes costs more than a $9.99 reader who lands it in five. The malcolms review is your evidence she's the second kind — cross-validated in the first session, no warm-up month required.

## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

Channeling has a shape, and you should know it going in: **she tunes in before she names things.** The first minutes of a session with a channeler are about establishing your energy — the specifics arrive after the link forms. If you need a reader firing named details from second one, the tarot-track readers on Kasamba will suit your temperament better; that's a fit question, not a quality flaw. Use the 3 free minutes exactly for this: if her rhythm is yours, you'll feel it before a cent is billed.

Second, about that 4.4 rating: her regulars openly argue it should be higher, and the review above shows why the decimal lags — it absorbs the sessions where a client wanted a different style, not the ones where the information was wrong. Read the reviews themselves; they're the better instrument.

## Should You Book Divine Princess?

**Book her if** your question is a *connection* question — is this person mine, are we past-life bound, why does the pull survive every breakup — and you want it read by a licensed channeler who treats soulmate theory as a discipline, cross-checks against what your other readers said, and charges less than a takeaway order to start.

**Skip her if** you want tool-forward sessions — a big spread narrated card by card — or if waiting through the tuning-in minutes will make you itchy. There are excellent spread-first readers on the platform; the right reader is the one whose format matches your question.

Either way, the audition costs almost nothing: 3 free minutes, then half price at $1.50/min. Ask her the connection question you've been carrying. If malcolms is right, what she says will match the truth you already half-know.
