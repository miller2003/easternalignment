The hardest style to find in psychic reading isn't accuracy — plenty of profiles claim it. It's the reader who can tell you a hard truth **without making you feel hit by it**. The industry mostly splits into two camps: the blunt truth-tellers (valuable, occasionally bruising) and the cushion-readers (comforting, occasionally vague). The middle is rare.

Psychic Advisor Sebastian lives in the middle, and his two-year client **Mir-Z** put the whole value proposition in three words that we'd frame if we could: **"honest yet gentle."**

Her full testimony, from his verified expert page, deserves the space: *"I have been consulting with Seb since last 2 years... I'd vouch for him. Go in with an open mind because he's honest yet gentle. And, a lot of predictions he had for me have come to pass, he picks up on things he couldn't have known as well... you're in good hands with Seb. Truly grateful for him, he is a gem. 💙"*

## The Lineage and the Toolkit

Sebastian's credentials read differently from most profiles because they're **structural** rather than mystical: a **third-generation psychic spiritualist**, a trained **Intuitive Life Coach**, and documented **coursework in remote viewing**. His ability stack covers all three clairs — clairsentience (feeling), clairaudience (hearing), clairvoyance (seeing) — which in practice means the same situation can arrive as emotion, as message, and as image, and he cross-checks one against the other.

Two consequences matter for you. First, the **mediumship**: he reads passed loved ones as comfortably as present ones, which makes his profile a two-in-one for clients whose question involves someone who has crossed. Second, the **life coach training**: his readings end in next steps, not just forecasts — the "what do I do with this" part that pure intuitives often skip.

His own words on the contract: *"What I tell you is always an honest account of what I see."* No drama, no hedging, no theater.

## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

Sebastian's gentleness has a **pace**, and the honest note is tempo: his sessions breathe. He is not the thirty-second-fireworks reader; he builds the read, checks it against what you're feeling, and delivers with care. If your instinct is to maximize named-details-per-minute, a speed-first profile will fit your stopwatch better — and Kasamba has several we've reviewed. His clients, by every review we verified, consider the pacing part of the kindness. Match your expectation accordingly.

Second, the voice rate ($39.99) is a genuine outlier for his tier. It's not a flaw — plenty of veterans price voice high — but if you only ever read by phone, the math will sting. The chat transcript gives you everything the call would, in writing.
