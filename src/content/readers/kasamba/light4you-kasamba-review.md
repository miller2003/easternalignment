## Kasamba's Founding Generation

Kasamba launched around the turn of the millennium. Jodee Lynn joined in **2002** — which makes her one of the platform's founding generation, a reader who has practiced professionally online for twenty-four years, longer than some of her clients have been alive.

Longevity on a per-minute platform is the harshest audit in this industry. There is no algorithm to hide behind, no way to delete a bad month: every session ends with the client deciding whether to return and what to write. The arithmetic that survived that audit: **56,253 readings, 15,276 likes against 242 dislikes — a 98.4% approval ratio.** Readers with thin talent churn out by year three, when the reviews catch up. Twenty-four years at 4.9 isn't branding. It's a ledger.

## The Kitchen-Table Tarot Education — Plus a Coaching Certificate

Her origin story is refreshingly unglamorous: *"Being captivated by tarot, I was privileged to learn tarot the old-fashioned way. Side by side, my friend's mother taught me how to weave a story using my psychic abilities and tarot. Most people call this the organic way of learning."*

No mystery-school diploma, no invented lineage — an apprenticeship at someone's kitchen table, the way the craft was transmitted for centuries before it was transmitted by webinar. But here's what Jodee added that her teacher couldn't have: **a certified life-coaching qualification**. *"As a certified life coach, I seamlessly integrate tarot and coaching techniques to offer accurate guidance."*

That fusion is the actual product, and it's worth understanding before you book. A pure tarot reader tells you what the cards show. A coach asks what you're going to *do* about it. In Jodee's sessions the two arrive fused — the prediction and the plan in the same minute. Her client **User_7326096** describes exactly this: *"Her insights have always helped me plan my future actions better and her predictions have been true."* Prediction tells you what's coming; coaching tells you how to meet it. She sells both.

## About Those Pauses — Why Slow Is the Product

Now the honest quirk, because you'll notice it in your first session and you should know what it is: **Jodee types slowly.** Watch the chat window and you'll see gaps — a minute here, two there — between your question and her answer. Her bio states the ground rule plainly: *"As you enter the chat room or phone systems, please at least have ten minutes available talk time."*

Here's the reframe the five-star regulars already understand: **the pause is the reading.** A scripted advisor answers instantly, because the script was written before you arrived. A reader who is actually pulling cards, laying them against your situation, and composing a coached response takes visible time. The typing indicator isn't dead air — it's the sound of the work happening. Budget the ten minutes she asks for, consider them depth insurance, and note that her per-minute rate makes even a generous session affordable: ten unhurried voice minutes cost about **$50**, and the first three are free.

There's also a budget hack almost nobody uses: her **email reading menu** — **$10 per question**, $50 for a general reading, $100 for a twelve-month forecast. A single focused question answered in writing for ten dollars, from a 24-year veteran, is quietly one of the best-value offers on Kasamba.
