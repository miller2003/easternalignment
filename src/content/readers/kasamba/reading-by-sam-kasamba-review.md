## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

Sam's format is **thorough**, which means measured — not instant. If your need is a one-sentence verdict in the first sixty seconds, his building style will feel slower than the firehose readers, and at his pace you'll want the session length to match the question. The 3 free minutes calibrate this quickly.

Second, the honest tenure note: 2021-origin means five years of volume, not twenty. The quality indicators (4.8 at 31k sessions, repeat clients, kind-and-thorough consensus) are exactly right for that age; just know the difference between "excellent young record" and "twenty-year institution" when you calibrate expectations.
