## The Dosing Philosophy — "Just the Right Amount"

Her bio states a reading philosophy you rarely see articulated: *"I provide just the right amount of insight, making it easy to my clients to understand and act on — while also observing and letting my clients know if there are any blockages."*

That's dose control. Many readers over-pour — everything they see, regardless of whether you can use it. Many under-pour — teasers that stretch the meter. Her stated method is the median: enough insight to act on, blockage flags included, no flood. It's a budget reader's superpower: at $1.99/min the incentive is volume, yet her design goal is efficiency.
