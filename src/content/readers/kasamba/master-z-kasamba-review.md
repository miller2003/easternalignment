Some profiles are easy to review. Master Z's demands the thing he himself asks for, in capital letters on his own profile: **"Look! Read all feedback!"**

So we did — all of it we could verify — and the honest summary is a profile that refuses to flatten into a slogan. Master Z is a **4th-generation psychic** who has been on Kasamba since **2002** — twenty-four years — with **29,782 readings** behind him. His thumb ledger is extraordinary: **13,631 likes against 168 dislikes**, a **98.8% positive ratio**. And yet his star average sits at **3.3**, far below the platform's elite. Both numbers are real. Both are current. Together they describe a reader whose *clients* — the people who actually book him — overwhelmingly approve, while the star average absorbs years of history and the occasional brutal miss.

That's not a defense; it's the honest arithmetic. His regulars' words do the rest.

## What the Veterans Say — and What They're Buying

The most complete endorsement in his feed comes from **User_4p3vh9**: *"Master Z has been my oracle for many years and gives the truth as is in short straight ways. it has helped more than I can ever co[unt]."* Parse the three commitments packed in there: many years (retention), truth as-is (style), short and straight (format). Master Z is not a luxury experience. He's a working oracle for people who want the answer without ceremony.

Then there's the review we've never seen elsewhere: **User_8332153**, returning after a two-year silence — *"I hadn't talked to him in almost 2 years and it was very much like just picking up where we left off. still very insightful."* A reader who holds your situation across a two-year gap isn't doing cold reads. That's a working relationship, resumed.

His own pitch matches the style: *"Master Psychic gives detailed reading, Good or bad, you get the Truth! Ready for the Truth?"* And *"Regulars pay less!"* — a veteran's pricing aimed at retention, not first-click dazzle.

## The Honest Complications

The same feed contains the counterweight, and his "read all feedback" invitation includes it. **Leah**: *"I don't recommend this reader. Common sense advice is there, but psychic, not so much. Also he was distracted."* **amin**: *"not happeing what he is saying."* Those are real experiences, and at a **3.3 star average** they're part of the record he asks you to weigh.

Our read of the pattern: Master Z delivers a **short, blunt, veteran-style read** that his long-term clients treat as an oracle — and that newcomers expecting a long, layered, exploratory session sometimes experience as curt or generic. The gap isn't fraud; it's fit. This is the profile where reading the feedback isn't due diligence theater — it's the actual selection mechanism, and he's built his pitch around it.

## The Price, Translated Into What You're Actually Buying

Here's the genuine oddity: **chat is $25.99/min but voice is $11.99/min** — the only profile in our entire batch where the phone is cheaper than typing. Given his short-straight format (better suited to voice anyway, per his clients' descriptions), the value path here is genuinely inverted: **the voice session is his value play.**

With the new-client promo — **3 free minutes + 50% off** — a first 15-minute voice session runs about **$60**: three minutes free, twelve at $6. At $25.99 chat, the same session would be $156. The math makes the format decision for you.

## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

The caveat is the entire architecture: **this is a polarizing premium profile, and the 3.3 is part of the purchase.** If you need certainty before booking, the market's aggregate confidence in him is lower than the veterans' individual conviction — that's the fact his star average encodes. Buy the free minutes and the feedback read, not the homepage.

Who should genuinely consider him: returning-style readers who want short, blunt sessions from a fourth-generation veteran, prefer voice, and can calibrate expectations from the mixed-but-loyal record. Who shouldn't: first-time seekers wanting a safe, polished, highly-rated experience — Kasamba's 4.9+ profiles at a third of the price serve that better, and we've reviewed several this month.

## Should You Book Master Z?

**Book him if** you are, specifically, the client his veterans describe — someone who wants the short, straight truth from a 24-year oracle they can resume after two years away, at a voice rate that undercuts his own chat.

**Skip him if** you want the platform's safest bets. That's not an insult to either side; it's the map his own feedback draws, and he'd be the first to point you to it.

Either way, he was **online at verification**, the free minutes apply, and the feedback is public by his own instruction. Read it — then decide whether the oracle your regulars describe is the oracle you need.
