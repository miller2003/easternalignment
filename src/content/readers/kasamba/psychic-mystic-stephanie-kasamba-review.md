## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

The caveat is her **supportive register**. Her sessions lead with support; the precision sits inside it. If your situation needs the cold verdict first — the no-cushion "it's over" school — her warmth will feel like padding. That's a fit question, not a flaw; her 20,000 satisfied clients are the counter-evidence.

Second: she was **offline at verification**. The twenty-year calendar manages itself — notify-me beats refreshing.
