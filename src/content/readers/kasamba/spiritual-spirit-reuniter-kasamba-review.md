## Why a Reunion Specialist Is a Different Instrument

Here's the distinction that matters when your heart is on the line.

A general love reader will tell you whether reconciliation is *likely*. A reunion specialist works the **process** — why the separation happened energetically, what's blocking the return, what the other person is actually feeling behind the silence, and what needs to shift in you for the door to open. It's closer to spiritual mediation than prediction. Her inclusion of **meditation therapy and prayer** in the toolkit isn't incidental; the client quotes show it's half the service.

Her formal background — she cites Higher Social Sciences and years of in-person consultations alongside her career — shows up in how she talks to people: structured, calm, and practical rather than dramatic.
