There are two ways to vet a psychic. The first is the one every review site uses: read the platform feed. The second is the one almost nobody bothers with: check whether the reader exists — by name, by brand, by reputation — *outside* the platform where you're about to spend money.

Psychic Tarot Andrew is the reader who made us do the second one.

On Kasamba, the numbers are already strong: **35,075 readings since 2008**, a **4.8 rating**, and **11,736 likes against 36 dislikes** — a **99.7% positive ratio** maintained across seventeen years. His bio is economical: *"Tarot Reader for over 20 years. Readings done with TLC."*

But Andrew doesn't only exist where the meter runs. He publishes at askpsychicdrew.com under his own name — Toronto-based, four decades of clairvoyant tarot, clients in 70 countries — and runs **live YouTube readings three nights a week**, plus a free online tarot tool anyone can try without paying him a cent. He has been featured in the Toronto Star and on podcasts. You can watch him work, for free, before you ever book. In an industry where anonymity is the default, that is an extraordinary amount of accountability to volunteer for.

## What "Readings Done With TLC" Actually Means

The phrase sounded like marketing until we read his feed alongside his site. Here's the through-line: Andrew's readings are **structured**. Classical tarot symbolism, spreads, timing — the Western tradition, delivered with what he calls TLC and what his clients describe, over and over, as *care about the outcome*.

His regulars don't talk about being stunned. They talk about being **informed and looked after**. One client, **User_7612193**, put it plainly: *"a wonderful person who wants me to succeed and be happy."* That's an unusual sentence to write about someone you've never met. It's also exactly what "with TLC" promises.

And underneath the warmth is a predictive record his clients actively document.

## The Receipts, According to the People Who Paid

We verified everything below from his live Kasamba review feed this month.

**TaraLee** is his most visible regular right now — five reviews in September alone, and her September 4th entry is the kind that makes review-reading worthwhile: *"Thanks for all your insights! They rock / I came back to add to this... everything in this read has now come to pass. It was spot on."* She read the session, waited, watched it land, **and came back to the review to say so**. That's the difference between a compliment and a documented result.

**Sai11**, another repeat client, kept it terse: *"As he said got contact as predicted."* In love-reading terms, that's the whole job: he said the person would reach out, and the person did.

**Lee Ann**: *"very inciteful as always."* The "as always" again — the vocabulary of years, not sessions.

The external record echoes the internal one. A client story published on his own site — originally an April 2025 Kasamba review — describes Andrew drawing what she calls the divorce card during a reading about her marriage: *"At the time I didn't believe it because I didn't want it to be true. Andrew was the only psychic that saw this — and low and behold, 8 months later my ex husband told me he wanted a divorce. I couldn't be happier!"* We flag the source honestly — it's self-published. But it matches the platform-side pattern: specifics, timestamps, and a client who returned to confirm.

## The Price, Translated Into What You're Actually Buying

Andrew charges **$9.99/min on chat and voice** — premium-tier, in line with his tenure and his 4.8. A focused 15-minute session is about **$150 at sticker**.

The new-client promo is where the decision gets rational: **3 free minutes + 50% off** puts your first 15 minutes at roughly **$90**, with the first three on the house. Given that TaraLee's documented case took one read to land, that's a defensible bet on a premium reader.

And here's the part we keep coming back to: **the free tier of Andrew's world is genuinely free.** Watch a Tuesday or Thursday or Sunday YouTube stream. Pull ten cards on his site's tool. If his interpretive voice resonates before you've paid anything, the promo session becomes a formality. Very few premium readers let you audition this thoroughly.

## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

Andrew is a **tarot formalist with a full calendar**. If you want a slow, rambling, open-ended session where the reader follows every tangent, his format — spreads, structure, timing — will feel disciplined to the point of brisk. He works like a professional with a queue, because he is one. For his regulars that's reassurance. For comfort-shoppers it can read as impersonal, and it's better you know that now than at minute four.

Second: the $9.99 rate is real money by the minute. He's the wrong reader for drifting — come with your question framed and the session pays for itself; come without one and you'll spend your promo minutes finding it.

## Should You Book Psychic Tarot Andrew?

**Book him if** you want tarot proper — structure, symbolism, timing — from a reader whose name, face, and forty-year reputation exist in public, and whose clients return to their own reviews to mark predictions as fulfilled.

**Skip him if** your budget is strictly $2-minute territory, or if you want slow, unstructured wandering. Neither is a flaw. Both are fit questions, and this review exists so you can answer them before the meter starts.

Either way, do the free audit first: catch one stream, pull one spread on his app, then use the 3 free minutes on Kasamba. If his style is yours — and TaraLee's five September reviews suggest it tends to stick — you'll know before the paid minute begins.
