## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

Melodie works from **your birth data and the transits**, and that shapes the session's feel. This is chart-and-cards work: layered, methodical, occasionally quieter than the clairvoyant highlight reel. If your fantasy of a reading is rapid-fire specifics from the first thirty seconds, a pure intuition reader will match your tempo better — and Kasamba has plenty. Her tempo buys you something else: readings that come with a *why* attached, which is why her clients stay for decades rather than sessions.

The second caveat is arithmetic: at $9.99/min she is a bad fit for open-ended, rambling sessions. Come with a real question. Her style rewards focus, and her regulars' reviews suggest she'll reward yours too.
