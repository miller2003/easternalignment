## Two Dislikes. Twenty-One Years.

Start with the number no other profile in this series can touch: **10,154 likes against 2 dislikes**. Not 97%, not 99.7% — effectively a perfect career, sustained since **2005** across **25,635 readings** and a 5.0-star platform average. Every other elite reader we've audited carries a negative column in the dozens or hundreds. Robyn's carries a rounding error.

Skeptic's pause, honestly taken: a two-dislike sample can't prove perfection — it can prove extraordinary selectivity, extraordinary skill, or both. What it does prove is this: across two decades and ten thousand rating events, the complaints that sink other careers (pace, accuracy, tone, ethics) have essentially never materialized publicly. In a profession where the negative column is the usual tell, its absence is the finding.

## The Business Practice — Priced Like It Means It

Her positioning is explicit: *"Top Business psychic... Accurate business forecast. Straight to the point. Convenient quick connect. **I do not waste your valuable time.** Immediate help for sensitive concerns. Complex issues, concise & confidential guidance."* The rates put her in consultant territory, and the practice is built to match: business forecasting, financial-spiritual advice, competitive edge, business coaching and evaluation, "War of Art" strategic flow, career life-path work.

The ethics screen is unusual and worth respecting: *"I cannot answer 'will I get the job' unless you are an established client. First, as a team we consider overall life goals to determine how an opportunity will enhance your future."* She's declining to optimize a decision she hasn't contextualized — the opposite of the fortune-teller reflex, and exactly what you want from $39.99/min counsel.

The delivery guarantee doubles as a technology claim: *"Your reading is unique. **I am the person typing to you — I do not use AI-generated chat.**"* In 2026, that sentence is itself a credential.
