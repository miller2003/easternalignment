The answer, from his own positioning and his clients' behavior, is the **deep-dive**: the reunion with layers, the relationship with a history, the question that has already defeated two or three cheaper readers. The caveat is arithmetic, not character: **he is the wrong reader for a tight budget.** There is no shame in that from either side — his rate is his positioning, and Kasamba's budget tier is genuinely good. If $144 for a first session is stress-money, the stress will sit in the session with you, and that's bad economics for everyone. Book him when the question justifies it; book the $2.99 tier when it doesn't.
