## The Name That Isn't One

Every reader on Kasamba performs branding: Mystic This, Divine That, Goddess of the Other. This reader registered the handle **"tarot"** — the literal, generic name of her tool. No mystique, no persona, no promise baked into a title. It's the spiritual equivalent of a restaurant called FOOD.

Here's why that's a signal rather than a gimmick: generic handles can't coast on brand. A reader named "tarot" is findable by everyone and memorable to no one — every one of her **47,813 readings since 2015** was earned by the session itself, not by a name that sells destiny. She describes herself with similar understatement: *"Gifted with clairvoyance & clairsentience... professional Psychic Intuitive and Empath."* Competence, stated plainly. The 4.9 rating across **12,930 likes against 488 dislikes** did the rest of the talking.
