### The "Everyone Has Intuitive Ability" Philosophy — Why It Matters

Golden Eye's bio on Kasamba is notably brief, but its core statement is worth examining: *"Everyone has plus or minus intuitive ability. Sometime rough time in life needs us to have assurance and patience."*

This framing positions her at a specific point on the spectrum of how psychic readers present themselves. Golden Eye's framing is firmly in the second camp. ## Nine Specialties in One Reader: How Golden Eye's Breadth Works in Practice

### The Full Specialist List — and What Each Covers

Golden Eye's nine active specialties are not a marketing checklist — each represents a distinct methodological tradition with its own tools, interpretive frameworks, and types of questions it is best suited to answer.

**Psychic Reading** is the broadest category — direct intuitive perception of a situation without a specific tool mediating the reading. **Tarot** works through the symbolic language of a card draw, mapping the 78-card deck's archetypes onto the client's situation. **Astrology** provides temporal and character context through planetary positions and transits. **Numerology** derives insight from the mathematical patterns in names and dates of birth. **Dream Analysis** interprets symbolic content from the client's unconscious. **Career** readings focus specifically on professional direction, timing, and decision points. **Fortune Telling** covers predictive guidance about future developments. **Mediumship** connects with deceased individuals to deliver messages or provide closure. And **Love and Relationships** encompasses the full range of romantic and relational questions.

What makes this list significant is not that Golden Eye lists all nine — it is that 21 years and 111,000 sessions suggest genuine working familiarity with each one across real client situations.

### Mediumship as a Distinct Offering: Connecting with Deceased Loved Ones

Mediumship deserves specific attention because it represents a categorically different service from the other eight specialties. Where most readings address living situations — relationships in progress, careers developing, decisions pending — mediumship addresses grief and connection with those who have died.

Clients seeking this type of reading are typically in a different emotional state than those seeking clarity on a relationship or career question. The need for a medium often arises from loss, unresolved conversations, or the desire for confirmation that a loved one is at peace. That requires a reader who can shift register entirely from the practical and predictive mode that serves most sessions into something more delicate and specific.

Golden Eye's inclusion of mediumship in her active practice, sustained across two decades, signals that she has experience with the full emotional range her clients bring — including the most difficult kind.

### Why Breadth Beats Specialisation for Clients Who Do Not Know What They Need

The standard argument for specialist readers is depth — a tarot reader who does nothing else has developed a granular understanding of the card system that a generalist cannot match. That argument holds when the client already knows which tool fits their question.

The problem is that many clients do not know. A person navigating the end of a relationship that also involves grief, career disruption, and a recurring dream pattern does not have a single-category question. A person wanting to connect with a deceased parent while also seeking clarity on a major life decision needs more than one method. In these cases, a reader who brings multiple systems to a session can respond to what actually emerges rather than working within the constraints of a single method.

Golden Eye's breadth means she can follow the client's situation wherever it goes — shifting from tarot to numerology to dream analysis within a single reading if the question requires it. That flexibility, built over 21 years, is the practical value proposition that the nine-specialty list encodes.
