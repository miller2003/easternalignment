## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

The caveat is the **format**: quick, direct, answer-shaped. If you want a long, immersive, exploratory session — the kind where the reader walks your whole situation with you — her efficiency will feel like acceleration where you wanted depth. Book a longer-form reader for that; her product is the clean answer at the clean price.

Second: the 4.8 is excellent but not elite, and the miss documented above is real. At this price, the rational strategy is hers and yours together: short sessions, specific questions, verify — and return if the answers keep landing.
