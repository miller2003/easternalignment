## What the Premium Actually Buys

Immense Love is a **natural-born clairvoyant** — her words: *"a clairvoyant who has the ability to see into your past, present and future that are not in the range of normal perception."* But the credential that shapes her sessions is her training as a **Spiritual Guidance Counselor and Energy Worker**, and the specialty she names twice: **Compatibility Readings** — *"focus on how a couple will navigate the twists and turns of life together."*

That's a different product than "will he text back." It's trajectory work: two people, their friction points, the seasons ahead, and what each of you does with them. Her bio frames the contract clearly: *"Even if the answers are difficult to hear, I connect with Divine Energy to help you move through the difficult space."* Difficult truths, delivered inside a framework built to make them usable.

Premium pricing at its best is not a tax — it's a filter. It buys longer arcs, deeper compatibility analysis, and a reader whose clients stay for years. Whether that's worth $16.99 a minute is the question this review exists to answer.

## The Price, Translated Into What You're Actually Buying

**Chat is $16.99/min; voice is $39.99/min.** The voice rate is among the steepest on the platform, and unless your situation specifically needs her voice, the math points one direction: chat, focused, with the transcript saved.

The new-client promo — **3 free minutes + 50% off** — is the one moment this profile becomes experimentally affordable: a first 15-minute session runs about **$102** instead of $255. That's a real purchase, so let's be honest about what it's for. This is not the reader for "quick gut check, $12 budget." This is the reader for the decision with consequences: the compatibility question before a big commitment, the couple-crossroads that three cheaper readers have already muddled.

If that's your situation, her veterans' arithmetic applies: one accurate trajectory read beats five vague ones — at any rate.

## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

The caveat is the price tag itself, said without apology: **she is expensive, and she's priced like it on purpose.** If your budget is tight, Kasamba's sub-$3 veterans (we've reviewed several this month) will serve a simple question better than a rushed session here. Booking Immense Love for a five-minute question is like hiring a tax attorney for a parking ticket — the talent is real, the fit is wrong.

Second: her approach is **counseling-paced**. Sessions breathe; she works the arc, not just the headline. Come with time budgeted, not stolen between meetings — the promo's 3 free minutes are your calibration window for her tempo.

## Should You Book Immense Love?

**Book her if** the question is big enough to deserve her — a compatibility decision, a couple trajectory, a connection where the wrong answer costs more than the session — and you want the reader whose four-year clients file confirmations like clockwork.

**Skip her if** you want a cheap, fast, single-question read tonight. That's not a downgrade; it's a match. Her rate exists for the sessions that need it.

Either way, she was online as we published — a window that closes quickly at her demand level. Use the 3 free minutes to test the connection, then decide whether the next twelve are an expense or, like her veterans concluded years ago, an investment with a paper trail.
