## How to Get the Most Out of an Ask Cristina Reading

### The Question Format That Activates Her Telepathic Process

Ask Cristina's readings respond best to questions anchored in a specific person. Give her the person's first name at the start of your question — not just "someone I've been seeing" but an actual name, which serves as her energetic focal point for the remote viewing process. Frame your question around understanding rather than prediction: "What is [name] feeling about our situation right now, and what's driving their behavior?" works far better than "Will [name] come back?" The first invites her telepathic access; the second pushes her toward prediction territory where her toolkit is less specifically suited.

### Using Kasamba's 3 Free Minutes With an Experienced Reader

Ask Cristina's experience means she orients quickly — you don't need to spend the free three minutes establishing context the way you might with a less seasoned advisor. State the person's name, give a one-sentence description of the situation, and ask your core question directly. Her initial response in those three minutes will tell you almost everything you need to know about whether the session is connecting. An experienced reader who gets a specific and accurate read within the first exchange is worth continuing with; if the opening response feels generic or question-heavy, that's useful calibration.

### When a Return Session Is Worth It — and When to Try Someone Else

Return sessions with Ask Cristina are particularly valuable for tracking an evolving situation over time — her telepathic approach produces a snapshot of a person's inner state at the moment of the reading, and returning weeks later to check that state against what actually unfolded is one of the most interesting ways to calibrate her accuracy for your specific situation. If the first session produced specific information that later checked out, a return session is well worth it. If the first session felt accurate in tone but vague in specifics, give the method one more session before switching; telepathic reading can take a session or two to find its focus with a new client.
