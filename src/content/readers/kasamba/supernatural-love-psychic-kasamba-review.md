There's a category on Kasamba most people scroll past with their hearts in their throats: **Cheating & Affairs**. It's the hardest question in love reading — not "does he love me" but "is he lying to me" — and it takes a particular kind of reader to work that lane full-time.

Supernatural Love Psychic — **SLP** to his regulars — is that reader. His platform categorization puts him there by trade: *"Is he/she cheating on you? Is he/she having an affair? I can reveal all the true information to help you."* Fifteen years and **29,147 readings** into his Kasamba career, the ledger stands at a **4.8** with **7,730 likes against 98 dislikes** — **98.7% positive** on a practice built from the questions nobody asks politely.

## What a Fourth-Generation Fidelity Reader Actually Does

His method stack is genuinely layered: clairvoyant mediumship (seeing), telepathic reading (feeling thoughts and emotions), and tarot (structuring the situation) — trained, per his bio, through meditation practice aimed at *"precisely connecting with human soul/mind/heart/spirit and current situations."* The fourth-generation lineage gives it the inherited-craft framing; the daily meditation practice gives it the discipline.

But the detail that separates him in this category is what he does **after** the reveal. His own words: *"I use special and success methods to heal/repair/re-unit any love & relationship matters."* The cheating question has two possible endings — the relationship ends or it's rebuilt — and a reader who only delivers the verdict leaves you stranded at the fork. SLP's practice includes the repair work: what to do, how to heal, whether restoration is worth attempting.

And the client testimony points at his actual product. **User_8168749**: *"I'm really glad to have found SLP. He's honest and always helps me focus on myself and what's most important."* Note the pivot — from the affair question to the self question. That's the sign of a reader who treats fidelity crises as clarity work, not just detective work.

## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

The caveat is the **category's nature**: fidelity reads end in answers that hurt when the answer is yes. SLP's style is honest-without-sugar-coating (his own words), and while his healing methods soften the aftermath, the reveal itself is the reveal. If your suspicion is a cloud you're not ready to have confirmed — wait, or book a general love reader and ask a broader question. Come to this practice when you're ready to know.

Second: the voice rate is functionally a format mandate. Chat, chat, chat.

## Should You Book Supernatural Love Psychic?

**Book him if** the fidelity question is the actual question — asked straight, answered straight, with restoration methods and self-focus guidance on the other side — from a fourth-generation reader whose years-long regulars prove the guidance kept working.

**Skip him if** you want gentleness-first or phone sessions, or if you're not ready for the answer. All three are legitimate; this page exists so the choice is conscious.

Either way, the audition is three free minutes, and the cost of knowing is about twenty-four dollars. Bring the situation, not the accusation. If cll's "years" are the pattern, you'll leave with the truth — and, if you want it, the path.
