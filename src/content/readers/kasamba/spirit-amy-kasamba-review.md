**Book Spirit Amy if** you want the most statistically validated reader we have ever put under the microscope — 49,727 readings since 2008, a perfect 5.0, and an approval ledger so clean it looks like a typo: 17,297 likes against 25 dislikes. **Skip her if** you're shopping the budget tier (her $12.99/min chat is a premium rate, and she earns it) or if you want card-by-card tarot theater — Amy's information arrives as symbols, pictures, and words she attributes to Spirit, delivered straight. Everyone else: the numbers below have no parallel in our audit series, and the story behind them is even better.

## The Ledger With Twenty-Five Smudges

Every reader on a per-minute platform collects dislikes. They're the tax on public work: mismatched expectations, clients angry at the message rather than the messenger, bad days. The best readers we've audited carry 96, 129, 208, 591 of them. It's simply what a decade of honest scorekeeping looks like.

Spirit Amy has **twenty-five**.

Sit with that. Eighteen years. 49,727 readings. And a lifetime tally of **17,297 likes against 25 dislikes — a 99.9% approval ratio** that stands alone in everything we've measured. You cannot buy that number, because Kasamba doesn't let readers delete it. You cannot charm your way to it, because charm fades by year three. The only way to hold 99.9% across eighteen years of paying strangers is to be right, kindly, almost every single time — and to decline the sessions where you can't be.

The clearest illustration hides in her recent feed. We pulled Amy's 150 most recent reviews and found exactly one marked negative. Here it is, in full: *"amazing as always the absolute best"* (**raza**, June 3). A loyal client, a glowing review, filed under the wrong thumb. Even her one-star bucket contains a compliment.

## 64% of Her Feed Comes From People Who Came Back

The second number is what explains the first. When we mapped Amy's 150 recent reviews by client name, **twenty different clients held three or more reviews each — 96 of the 150, nearly two-thirds of her visible feed**. **reeface** holds thirteen. **Princess40** holds twelve. Nobody holds thirty, which matters: this isn't a fan club with one obsessive benefactor, it's a broad population of people who each independently decided, a dozen times over, to pay again.

Listen to what the long-timers actually say. **Cris1978**, who has read with Amy for years (August 16): *"She never tells you what she thinks you want to hear. She tells you exactly what she sees, but explains it with so much clarity and kindness. I always leave our sessions feeling lighter, reassured, and with a much better understanding of whatever situation I'm dealing with."* That's the whole retention machine in one quote: honesty as the product, kindness as the delivery, lightness as the outcome.

**robotshavehearts** (July 15) frames it as companionship through hard years: *"She has been in my life during some tough moments. She steers me onto the right path... It's rare to have a real human being with a heart of gold guiding you along this journey."* And **M.Colorado** (May 31) captures the session tempo: *"you are so fast because I am always throwing questions out to you before you can even answer them."* Fast, warm, honest — the three traits that make a reader into a habit.

## The Medium Who Volunteers

Amy's own description of her method is unusually precise for this industry: *"My ability as a medium is clairvoyance — 'clear seeing.' I see symbols, pictures or spirits in the mind's eye with the physical eyes. I am a clairaudient, which means I will hear words that Spirit is saying."* Two channels — images and words — and no cards required. Whether you read that as gift or as a veteran's pattern-matching, the receipts it produces are concrete. **kelly** (July 25) returned specifically to confirm one: *"I came to update what she said. she gave me a date and description of meeting someone and it happened exactly."* A date. A description. Then reality, matching both.

And then there's the part of Amy's résumé that doesn't appear in her Kasamba metrics at all. Third-party directories that rank mediums — not affiliate copy, but editorial reviews — credit her with **volunteering her time to aid families in missing-person cases**, and describe her years working the New England area as a medium and psychic development teacher. We flag any claim we can't verify ourselves, so treat those as reported, not audited. But they align with something we *can* see in her feed: mediumship clients describing closure. **PamShadowRat** (June 21): *"she made me feel better — actually gave me the closure I've been dying for. and I believe her because she is 1000% accurate and a very very very good psychic medium. You need not go to anyone else if you want 100% accuracy."* People don't write sentences like that about a love prediction. They write them when someone helps them say goodbye.

## How to Book Her Smart

The pricing decision is simple. **Chat is $12.99/min with 3 free minutes for new clients; voice is $25.49/min.** Book chat — it's half the price, and her symbol-and-words delivery actually benefits from text, because every image she describes lands in a transcript you can revisit when it starts matching reality.

The honest math: after your 3 free minutes, a focused 10-minute session runs about **$91**; fifteen minutes, about **$156**. That's a premium spend, so make it a premium session: one question that matters, asked cleanly in your free minutes — *"What do you see around this relationship?"* or *"Is there anything you can tell me about my grandmother?"* Amy also lists **palm readings** among her specialties, a rare second modality on the platform, for clients who want their own hands read rather than a situation.

One timing note: her regulars are legion and her hours are finite. If she's offline, use Kasamba's notify feature rather than refreshing — the clients holding thirteen reviews learned long ago that she's worth the wait.

## The Small Print — What the Premium Buys

Three things to know, none of which touch the product. First: **$12.99/min is not a budget rate**, and we won't pretend otherwise. The counter-argument is the ledger — 25 dislikes in eighteen years is what the premium buys, and the 3 free minutes let you verify the fit before a single paid one ticks. Second: **voice is priced at nearly double chat.** Her entire regular population lives in text; join them. Third: **her readings don't look like a card spread.** No laid-out tarot theater — she reports what she sees and hears, and explains the symbols as they arrive. Clients who want ritual aesthetics should know the format going in; clients who want the date-and-description receipts know which format produced them.
