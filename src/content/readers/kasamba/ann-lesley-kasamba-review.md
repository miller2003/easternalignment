The most unusual thing on Ann Lesley's profile isn't the credential wall — though the wall is real. It's a warning she writes to you, in her own words, about her own sessions:

*"Please be aware that when you talk to me time can go so fast and before you realize you have clocked up more money than what you intended to spend... limit yourself to how many minutes you do want to speak to me... ask me to tell you when you have spoken for the minutes that you want and then I will disconnect you."*

Sit with that for a moment. A per-minute reader — whose income scales with your minutes — volunteering a **consumer-protection notice** about her own sessions, and offering to cut you off at your budget on request. That is the opposite of the industry's worst stereotype, and it's the first signal that the 99.7% approval ratio behind her name wasn't an accident.

The numbers: **31,497 readings since 2006** — twenty years on Kasamba — with **15,579 likes against 42 dislikes**. We've audited a lot of ledgers this season; this is one of the cleanest ever attached to a two-decade tenure.

Translation, for the practical reader: Ann brings **counselling structure** to her readings. Her specialty list matches: psychic readings with numerology and dream analysis, love, and career guidance — the full life-map menu.

## The Price, Translated Into What You're Actually Buying

**Chat and voice are both $12.99/min** — flat, no mode markup. With Kasamba's new-client promo — **3 free minutes + 50% off** — a first 15-minute session runs about **$78**: three minutes free, twelve at roughly $6.50.

Given the credential wall and the two-decade ledger, that's mid-premium money, and her own advice (the warning we opened with) is the correct way to spend it: **set your minute limit before you connect and tell her.** She'll honor it. That single habit turns a premium session into a controlled experiment — and per susie and Ben, the details arrive quickly enough that a controlled session is all most questions need.

## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

Ann's style is **warm and positive-forward** — reassurance is a documented feature of her sessions ("always positive," "reassuring" recur across reviews). If your situation needs the cold verdict delivered without anesthetic — the "no, it's over, move on" school of reading — her manner will feel cushioned by design. That's a fit question, not a flaw: her regulars explicitly prize the warmth. Know which night you're having.

Second: the rate. At $12.99 she sits above the budget tier, and the 99.7% ledger is exactly what you're paying for. If the budget binds, Kasamba's $2-3 veterans serve simpler questions well.

## Should You Book Ann Lesley?

**Book her if** you want fast, warm, credentialed guidance — a counsellor's structure behind a born psychic's sight, from a reader whose own profile protects your wallet and whose 42 dislikes in fifteen thousand ratings speak louder than any bio.

**Skip her if** you want the budget tier, or if tonight requires the unsweetened verdict first and comfort later. Both exist on Kasamba; the fit is yours to choose.

Either way, do what her profile asks: set the limit, ask her to hold you to it, and use the 3 free minutes to feel the speed. If Ben and SJ are the norm, you'll understand why twenty years of clients kept the meter running — carefully.
