## The One Honest Caveat — and Who It's Actually a Warning For

Nicola's style is **untheatrical**. No costumes, no doom-and-gloom drama, no ceremony — just fast, honest, accurate, in her own words. If your idea of a reading includes incense-level drama and pronouncements from the void, her sessions will feel like a warm, precise conversation. That's a feature for the quest-clients; a mismatch for the theater-seekers. Know which one you are.

Second: she was **offline at verification** — the only real friction at this price. Use the notify-me button; twenty-four years of clients have the alert trained.
