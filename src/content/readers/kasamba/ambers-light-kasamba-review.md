## The Most Honest Sentence on Kasamba

Every review site has a paragraph about advisor honesty. Ambers Light's bio does the work itself, twice:

*"If I am not shown the answer that you seek then I will not lie to you just to appease you."*

**Gentle is the house style.** If you want machine-gun answers and hard deadlines, her pace will feel soft. If you want to be *met* — especially in grief — soft is precisely the point.
