How We Tested and Ranked

Four signals, in order of how hard they are to fake.

1. Session volume as survival evidence. A reader with 50,000+ completed readings on a single platform has survived entirely on repeat business. Nobody sustains 50,000 sessions on one lucky first impression — the platform’s own economics kill inaccurate readers within months, because clients who feel burned do not rebook. Volume at a sustained high rating is the closest thing this industry has to audited accounts.

2. Negative-review forensics. We read the 1–2 star reviews for every candidate. The content of complaints is more informative than their count: “she told me he was not coming back and I was furious” is often delayed evidence of accuracy, while “she kept me on the meter for twelve minutes saying nothing” is a structural problem no promo fixes. Readers whose negative reviews describe meter-running or vagueness were excluded regardless of star rating.

3. Promo-price structure analysis. Kasamba’s new-client pricing (3 free minutes with every new advisor, plus 50% off the first paid session) means the price you actually pay is not the sticker price. We rank by the real cost of a verified first session — and we flag every reader whose post-promo regular rate changes the long-term math.

4. Methodological transparency. Readers who state what they are doing — “the Three of Swords in the near-future position,” “I am reading his current emotional state directly” — produce auditable claims you can check weeks later. Vague-output readers produce nothing checkable, and uncheckable readings are where the industry’s bad reputation comes from.

What the New-Client Deal Actually Lets You Do

Kasamba’s structure is the most audition-friendly of the three major platforms: 3 free minutes with every new advisor, plus 50% off your first paid session.   1. Write your question before you open the chat. One sentence, specific, with the person’s first name if relevant. Vague questions get vague readings at any price.
