Perth, Australia: The Timezone Feature North American Clients Are Missing

Sophia Rose Light is based in Perth, Western Australia — the only reader in this eleven-review series who is not in North America. Perth operates on AWST (Australian Western Standard Time), which is UTC+8. This puts her 13 hours ahead of US Eastern time and 16 hours ahead of US Pacific time. The practical implication is immediately useful: when it is 2 AM in New York and a client cannot sleep because their relationship situation is consuming them, Sophia Rose Light is sitting in Perth at 3 PM, in the middle of her working day, fully available.

When Sophia Is Available vs. When North American Readers Are Available

The inverse schedule dynamic works in both directions. When North American readers are at peak availability — US Eastern evenings between 7 PM and midnight — it is between 8 AM and 1 PM in Perth, which may be outside Sophia’s primary working window. But when North American clients are in the hours that crisis most frequently strikes — late night, early morning, the 3 AM spiral of anxiety that no daytime session can pre-empt — she is the reader who is awake, present, and available in real time. For clients who have ever needed a reading urgently and found every reader they trusted showing as offline, this timezone inversion is not a scheduling inconvenience. It is a genuine service gap being filled by a geographically specific advisor who happens to be active when the demand is highest and the supply is lowest.

What Shamanic Healing Brings to a Psychic Reading That Clairvoyance Alone Cannot

Standard clairvoyant and Tarot readings work at the level of current energy — reading what is present in a situation, what is likely to unfold, what the client’s energy field reveals about their relationship to the circumstance. Shamanic healing operates at a different layer of the energetic body: it works with soul fragments, extraction of intrusive energies, restoration of power that has been lost through trauma or violation, and connection to helping spirits and ancestral lines. A shamanic practitioner bringing this framework into a psychic session is not just reading what is happening — she is assessing what has been disrupted at the soul level and what is needed for restoration. For clients whose situations involve deep trauma, long-standing stuck patterns, or what feels like energetic interference in their lives and relationships, the shamanic layer of Sophia’s practice reaches places that card reading and clairvoyance structurally cannot.

An Akashic Records reading does not answer will this relationship work out. It answers why does this type of relationship keep appearing in your life, what karmic contract is operating here, and what would resolution at the soul level look like. This is a fundamentally different service from predictive reading, and it requires a client who is ready to receive that level of depth. Sophia also notes that sessions often activate Kundalini energy — the primal spiritual force that, when awakened, moves through the body’s energy centers and can produce profound shifts in awareness, perception, and life orientation.
